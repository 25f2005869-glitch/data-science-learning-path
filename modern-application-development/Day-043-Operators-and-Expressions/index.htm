Operators and Expressions

Day 043 — JavaScript Fundamentals

1. What Are Operators?

Operators perform operations on values called operands.

10 + 20

Operator: + | Operands: 10 and 20 | Result: 30

2. Arithmetic Operators

+

Addition

10 + 5 = 15

−

Subtraction

10 - 5 = 5

*

Multiplication

10 * 5 = 50

/

Division

10 / 5 = 2

%

Remainder

10 % 3 = 1

**

Exponentiation

2 ** 3 = 8

3. Assignment Operators

let score = 80;
score += 10;
score -= 5;
score *= 2;
score /= 2;
Compound assignment operators update an existing variable.

4. Comparison Operators

==

Loose Equality

5 == "5" → true

===

Strict Equality

5 === "5" → false

>

Greater Than

10 > 5 → true

<

Less Than

10 < 5 → false

>=

Greater or Equal

10 >= 10 → true

!==

Strict Inequality

5 !== "5" → true

5. Logical Operators

&&

AND

Both conditions must be true.

||

OR

At least one condition must be true.

!

NOT

Reverses a Boolean value.

age >= 18 && hasID === true

6. Ternary Operator

The ternary operator provides a compact way to select between two expressions.

age >= 18 ? "Adult" : "Minor"

8. Operator Precedence

10 + 5 * 2 → 20

(10 + 5) * 2 → 30

Parentheses can be used to explicitly control the order of evaluation.
