# 📘 Day 075 — Nudge Economics: Consumer Behavior in E-Commerce

## 🎯 Objective

- Understand nudges in e-commerce.
- Measure consumer conversion behaviour.
- Compare nudge conditions with a No Nudge baseline.

## 🧾 Case Note

- The dataset is synthetic and illustrative.
- It is not official IITM data.
- It is not real consumer data.
- It is designed for business-data practice.

## 🧮 Metrics

- Conversion Rate = purchases / visits × 100.
- Click Rate = clicks / exposures × 100.
- Lift = nudge conversion rate − baseline conversion rate.
- Revenue per Visit = order value × purchase flag.
