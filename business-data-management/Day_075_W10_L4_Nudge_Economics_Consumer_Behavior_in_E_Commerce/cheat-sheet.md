# ⚡ Day 075 Cheat Sheet — Nudge Economics

## 🔢 Formulas

- Conversion Rate % = Purchases / Visits × 100.
- Click Rate % = Clicks / Exposures × 100.
- Lift pp = Nudge Conversion % − Baseline Conversion %.
- Revenue per Visit = Order Value × Purchase Flag.

## 💡 Nudge Concepts

- Default → suggested choice.
- Free Shipping → delivery benefit message.
- Social Proof → popularity information.
- Limited Time → scarcity framing.
- No Nudge → control condition.

## 📊 Metrics

- Visits.
- Exposures.
- Clicks.
- Purchases.
- Conversion Rate.
- Click Rate.
- Order Value.
- Lift in percentage points.

## 🧠 Baseline

- Find No Nudge.
- Calculate its conversion rate.
- Use it as the reference.
- Keep the denominator definition consistent.

## 🐼 Pandas

- read_excel loads the workbook.

- groupby creates groups.
- agg calculates summaries.
- to_csv exports results.

## 📈 Charts

- Doughnut → exposure composition.
- Column → nudge comparison.
- Line → regional comparison.
- Scatter → two numeric variables.

## 🔍 Checks

- Check denominator.
- Check sample size.
- Check units.
- Check baseline.
- Check causal assumptions.
- Separate percentage points from relative percentages.

## ⚠️ Avoid

- Do not treat synthetic results as real evidence.
- Do not claim causation from a simple comparison.
- Do not confuse clicks with purchases.
- Do not compare percentages with different definitions.

## 🧪 Example

- 20% conversion versus 15% baseline gives 5 percentage-point lift.
- Relative growth is a different calculation.
- Always identify the metric being reported.
