## 📌 Day 044 — Portfolio Management Working

**Course:** Business Data Management (BDM)

**Lecture:** W7_L7 Portfolio management working

**Dataset:** Illustrative ACE Gears-style manufacturing dataset

This day focuses on product portfolio analysis using business data.

The working connects revenue, production, sales, cost and margin.

The objective is to compare products using measurable indicators.

All numerical data in this package is illustrative and created for learning.

### 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand product portfolio working.
- Calculate product revenue.
- Calculate gross margin.
- Calculate margin percentage.
- Calculate revenue share.
- Compare production and sales.
- Calculate sales conversion.
- Calculate scrap rate.
- Rank products.
- Present portfolio information clearly.

### 📊 Core Metrics

- Revenue = total sales value.
- Gross Margin = Revenue − Production Cost.
- Margin % = Gross Margin ÷ Revenue.
- Revenue Share % = Product Revenue ÷ Total Revenue.
- Sales Conversion % = Sales Units ÷ Production Units.
- Scrap Rate % = Scrap Units ÷ Production Units.

### 🔍 Working Approach

Start with the raw data.

Group observations by product.

Aggregate revenue, cost, production and sales.

Calculate gross margin and percentages.

Compare products side by side.

Interpret charts using calculated evidence.

### 💡 Interpretation Principle

Revenue shows scale of sales contribution.

Gross margin shows absolute margin contribution.

Margin percentage shows margin relative to revenue.

Revenue share shows portfolio concentration.

Sales conversion and scrap rate provide operating context.

### 📚 Detailed Notes

Portfolio management working converts product records into a comparable portfolio view.

Revenue measures scale of contribution.

Gross margin measures absolute contribution after production cost.

Margin percentage normalizes margin by revenue.

Revenue share describes contribution to total portfolio revenue.

Sales conversion compares sales units with production units.

Scrap rate compares scrap units with production units.

A portfolio presentation should state the metric, comparison and evidence.
