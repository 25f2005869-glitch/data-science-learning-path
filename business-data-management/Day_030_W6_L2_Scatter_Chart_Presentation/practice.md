## Business Cases

- Case 1: Ad spend and revenue rise together. What can you say?
- Case 2: One point has very high revenue. What should you investigate?
- Case 3: Correlation is positive. Can you claim advertising caused revenue?
- Case 4: A product has high revenue but low ad spend. What question could follow?
- Case 5: Management asks for one relationship chart. Why might scatter be useful?
- Case 6: The points are widely dispersed. How should the relationship be described?
- Case 7: A point is far from the general pattern. What should be checked?
- Case 8: Revenue is high but units are low. What additional analysis could help?

## Presentation Drill

- Explain the business question in 15 seconds.
- Explain X-axis in 10 seconds.
- Explain Y-axis in 10 seconds.
- Describe the pattern in 30 seconds.
- Explain correlation in 20 seconds.
- Give one limitation.
- Give one business implication.
- Avoid saying that correlation proves causation.
