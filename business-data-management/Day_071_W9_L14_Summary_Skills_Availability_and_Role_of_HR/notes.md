# 📝 Day 071 Notes — Skills Availability and Role of HR

## 1. Skills Inventory
A skills inventory records capabilities available across a workforce.

## 2. Skill Record
A skill record connects an employee with a particular capability.

## 3. Skill Score
A skill score is a numerical practice measure representing assessed capability.

## 4. Skill Availability
Skill availability indicates whether a capability is represented in the analysed records.

## 5. Availability Rate
Availability rate expresses available records as a percentage of the relevant population.

## 6. Skill Gap
A skill gap indicates a difference between required or expected capability and observed capability.

## 7. Gap Flag
A gap flag converts a threshold rule into an analyzable indicator.

## 8. Department Analysis
Department analysis shows how capability patterns differ across organisational units.

## 9. Regional Analysis
Regional analysis provides geographic context to workforce capabilities.

## 10. Role Analysis
Role analysis connects skills with job responsibilities.

## 11. Experience Analysis
Experience-level analysis can reveal differences in capability coverage.

## 12. Training Hours
Training hours provide a development-related variable.

## 13. Training Need
A training need flag can identify records requiring further development review.

## 14. Workforce Planning
Workforce planning connects current capabilities with future organisational requirements.

## 15. Skills Planning
Skills planning focuses on which capabilities are available, missing or developing.

## 16. HR Analytics
HR analytics turns workforce data into measurable summaries.

## 17. HR Skills Inventory
HR can maintain structured records of workforce skills.

## 18. Gap Identification
HR can use skill-gap analysis to locate development areas.

## 19. Training Planning
Skill-gap information can support targeted learning plans.

## 20. Internal Mobility
Skill profiles can support structured internal movement analysis.

## 21. Succession Planning
Skills data can provide one input into succession planning.

## 22. Performance Context
Performance scores can be reviewed alongside skills without assuming causation.

## 23. Dashboard
A dashboard provides a compact view of selected capability metrics.

## 24. KPI
A KPI summarises an important workforce measure.

## 25. Availability KPI
Availability rate shows the percentage of records meeting the availability condition.

## 26. Gap KPI
Gap rate shows the percentage of records meeting the illustrative gap condition.

## 27. Threshold
A threshold defines the rule used to classify a record.

## 28. Data Quality
Employee identifiers should be unique.

## 29. Missing Skills
Missing skill values require review before calculating coverage.

## 30. Duplicate Employees
Duplicate employee records can distort workforce counts.

## 31. Consistent Categories
Department and role names should use consistent labels.

## 32. Score Scale
Skill scores should use a documented scale.

## 33. Training Interpretation
Training hours describe exposure to training; they do not alone prove skill improvement.

## 34. Descriptive Analysis
The workbook describes the illustrative dataset.

## 35. Causation
A difference between groups does not automatically establish why the difference exists.

## 36. HR Decision Support
HR analytics can support investigation and planning.

## 37. Privacy
Real employee skill data should be appropriately protected.

## 38. Governance
Thresholds and definitions should be documented.

## 39. Reproducibility
The Python script provides a reproducible analysis workflow.

## 40. Final Learning
Skills availability analysis connects workforce data with practical HR planning.
