## 📊 Day 045 — Product Portfolio Presentation

**Course:** Business Data Management (BDM)

**Lecture:** W7_L8 Product portfolio presentation

**Programme:** IIT Madras BS Degree — Diploma Level

**Dataset:** Illustrative ACE Gears-style manufacturing dataset

### 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Convert product analysis into presentation-ready outputs.
- Present product revenue contribution.
- Present gross margin contribution.
- Present margin percentage.
- Present revenue share.
- Compare production and sales.
- Use sales conversion as supporting evidence.
- Use scrap rate as operational context.
- Build a logical presentation sequence.
- Separate descriptive evidence from recommendations.

### 📊 Main Metrics

- Revenue
- Gross Margin
- Margin %
- Revenue Share %
- Production Units
- Sales Units
- Sales Conversion %
- Scrap Rate %

### ⚡ Presentation Cheat Sheet

Revenue = SUM of product revenue.

Gross Margin = Revenue − Production Cost.

Margin % = Gross Margin / Revenue.

Revenue Share % = Product Revenue / Total Revenue.

Sales Conversion % = Sales Units / Production Units.

Scrap Rate % = Scrap Units / Production Units.

### Which Visual?

Revenue comparison → bar/column chart.

Gross margin comparison → bar/column chart.

Revenue share comparison → bar/column chart.

Production vs sales → clustered column chart.

Headline totals → KPI cards.

Exact metrics → table.

### Presentation Formula

Business Question → Metric → Visual → Evidence → Observation.

### Good Presentation Statements

State the metric being compared.

State the products being compared.

Use a number or percentage as evidence.

Do not confuse revenue with margin.

Do not confuse gross margin with margin percentage.

Do not interpret scrap rate as revenue performance.

### Excel Checks

Check percentage number formats.

Check chart titles.

Check product labels.

Check that all three products appear.

Check that revenue share totals approximately 100%.

Check formulas reference the correct source sheet.

### Python Checks

Check the input workbook path.

Check that Portfolio_Data exists.

Check that outputs folder is created.

Check generated CSV.

Check generated PNG charts.

### Presentation Sequence

1. Portfolio overview.
2. Revenue comparison.
3. Gross margin comparison.
4. Margin percentage comparison.
5. Revenue-share comparison.
6. Production versus sales.
7. Evidence summary.

### Dashboard

Use KPI cards for:

- Total Revenue
- Total Gross Margin
- Overall Margin %
- Total Sales Units

Use charts for product comparisons.

### Key Rule

One chart should answer one clear question.

Do not add a chart only because data is available.
