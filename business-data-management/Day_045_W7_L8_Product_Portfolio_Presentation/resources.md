### Suggested Practice Workflow

Step 1: Review raw data.

Step 2: Verify product calculations.

Step 3: Identify the headline metrics.

Step 4: Choose one visual for each question.

Step 5: Write one evidence statement for each visual.

Step 6: Arrange the visuals in a logical order.

Step 7: Present the dashboard aloud.

Step 8: Recreate the analysis in Python.

### Presentation Skills

Keep titles specific.

Keep labels readable.

Use consistent units.

Use consistent percentage formatting.

Do not overload one chart with unrelated measures.

Use a table when exact values matter more than visual comparison.

Use a chart when comparison matters more than exact values.
