## 📊 Day 045 — Product Portfolio Presentation

**Course:** Business Data Management (BDM)

**Lecture:** W7_L8 Product portfolio presentation

**Programme:** IIT Madras BS Degree — Diploma Level

**Dataset:** Illustrative ACE Gears-style manufacturing dataset

### 🎯 Practice Objectives

- Interpret product revenue.
- Interpret product margins.
- Explain revenue share.
- Compare production and sales.
- Select appropriate charts.
- Build a presentation sequence.
- Write evidence-based observations.

### 📊 Metrics to Practice

- Revenue
- Gross Margin
- Margin %
- Revenue Share %
- Production Units
- Sales Units
- Sales Conversion %
- Scrap Rate %

### 🧪 Practice Questions

1. What is total portfolio revenue?

2. What is total portfolio gross margin?

3. Which product contributes the most revenue?

4. Which product has the largest revenue share?

5. Which product has the largest gross margin?

6. Which product has the highest margin percentage?

7. Do revenue and gross-margin rankings tell the same story?

8. What does revenue share add to the revenue chart?

9. Why should margin percentage be shown with gross margin?

10. Which product has the highest sales conversion?

11. Which product has the highest scrap rate?

12. How would you explain production versus sales?

13. Which chart should appear first in the presentation?

14. Which metrics belong in KPI cards?

15. Which metric is best for portfolio concentration?

16. Write one sentence explaining the revenue chart.

17. Write one sentence explaining the margin chart.

18. Write one sentence explaining the revenue-share chart.

19. Write one sentence explaining production versus sales.

20. Write three closing observations using numerical evidence.

### Mini Presentation Task

Prepare a five-part presentation.

Part 1: Portfolio overview.

Part 2: Revenue contribution.

Part 3: Margin contribution.

Part 4: Revenue mix and operations.

Part 5: Evidence summary.
