# 🧪 Day 058 Practice — Introduction to HR as a Function

## Practice Setup

Open the Excel workbook.

Start from HR_Data.

Do not change the raw data before checking it.

Record your answers in a separate copy if needed.

## Task 1 — HR Basics

Define HR in your own words.

List five common HR activities.

Explain why HR is a business function.

Give two examples of workforce data.

## Task 2 — Headcount

Find total employee count.

Calculate headcount by department.

Calculate headcount by region.

Calculate headcount by employment type.

## Task 3 — Compensation

Calculate average salary.

Calculate average salary by department.

Calculate average salary by region.

Identify the department-level salary variation without assuming a cause.

## Task 4 — Performance

Calculate average performance.

Calculate average performance by department.

Compare each department with the overall average.

Write two factual observations.

## Task 5 — Attrition

Count attrition cases.

Calculate overall attrition rate.

Calculate attrition rate by department.

Calculate attrition rate by region.

Explain why a small dataset needs cautious interpretation.

## Task 6 — Training

Calculate average training hours.

Compare training hours by department.

Create a bar chart of training hours by department.

Write one descriptive insight.

## Task 7 — Attendance

Calculate average attendance.

Compare attendance by department.

Check whether any attendance value looks unusual.

Explain why attendance alone cannot explain performance.

## Task 8 — Dashboard

Review all four workbook charts.

Check chart titles and axes.

Trace each chart back to its source table.

Write three observations supported by the data.

## Task 9 — Python

Run introduction_to_hr_as_a_function.py.

Open the generated CSV summaries.

Open the generated PNG charts.

Compare Python outputs with Excel summaries.

## Task 10 — Business Thinking

Why might HR track headcount?

Why might HR track training?

Why might HR track attrition?

What additional data would improve an attrition analysis?

Why should employee data be handled carefully?

## Mini Case

A company notices different attrition rates across departments.

List three descriptive checks you would perform.

List three additional variables you would want.

Write a short note explaining why the pattern does not by itself establish a cause.

## Task 11 — Excel Formula Practice

Use COUNT or COUNTA for headcount.

Use AVERAGE for salary.

Use COUNTIF for department counts.

Use AVERAGEIF for department-level salary.

Use COUNTIF for attrition cases.

Calculate the overall attrition percentage.

## Task 12 — Group Comparison

Compare Sales and Finance.

Compare HR and IT.

Compare Operations and Marketing.

Record headcount for each group.

Record average salary for each group.

Record average performance for each group.

## Task 13 — Data Quality

Check for missing employee IDs.

Check for duplicate employee IDs.

Check department spelling.

Check employment-type categories.

Check attendance percentages.

Check performance-score values.

## Task 14 — Visualization

## Task 15 — Interpretation

Write three factual observations.

Write two questions for further analysis.

Write one limitation of the dataset.
