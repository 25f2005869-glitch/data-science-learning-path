# 📝 Day 072 Notes — Introduction to Finance Industry and FinTech

## 1. Finance Industry
The finance industry provides services related to money, credit, risk, investment and payments.

## 2. Financial Institution
A financial institution provides one or more financial services.

## 3. Commercial Bank
Commercial banks provide banking services such as deposits, credit and payments.

## 4. NBFC
An NBFC provides specified financial activities without operating as a traditional commercial bank.

## 5. Insurance
Insurance transfers or manages financial risk through insurance contracts.

## 6. Capital Markets
Capital markets facilitate raising and trading financial capital.

## 7. Payments
Payment systems enable transfer and settlement of funds.

## 8. Investment Services
Investment services support allocation and management of capital.

## 9. FinTech
FinTech describes technology-enabled financial products, services and processes.

## 10. Digital Payments
Digital payments use electronic channels to move money.

## 11. Digital Lending
Digital lending applies technology to parts of the credit process.

## 12. WealthTech
WealthTech applies technology to investment and wealth-management activities.

## 13. InsurTech
InsurTech applies technology to insurance processes and services.

## 14. RegTech
RegTech applies technology to regulatory and compliance activities.

## 15. Finance Data
Financial businesses generate transaction, customer, revenue and operational data.

## 16. Customer Data
Customer counts can be used to describe service reach.

## 17. Transaction Data
Transaction counts can describe digital activity.

## 18. Transaction Value
Transaction value measures the monetary amount associated with transactions.

## 19. Digital Adoption
Digital adoption measures the proportion or level of digital usage under a defined metric.

## 20. Revenue
Revenue is a business-scale measure.

## 21. Revenue per Employee
Revenue per employee relates revenue to workforce size.

## 22. Regional Analysis
Regional grouping provides geographic context.

## 23. Service Analysis
Service grouping allows comparison of different financial activities.

## 24. Institution Analysis
Institution type analysis compares broad categories of financial organisations.

## 25. FinTech Analysis
FinTech category analysis compares different technology-enabled finance activities.

## 26. KPI
A KPI summarises an important business measure.

## 27. Dashboard
A dashboard combines selected metrics and visualisations.

## 28. Doughnut Chart
A doughnut chart can show composition among institution types.

## 29. Column Chart
A column chart can compare revenue across FinTech categories.

## 30. Line Chart
A line chart can show an ordered sequence such as regional values.

## 31. Scatter Plot
A scatter plot can examine the relationship between two numerical variables.

## 32. Data Dictionary
A data dictionary defines fields and units.

## 33. Data Quality
Numeric fields should be validated before calculations.

## 34. Category Consistency
Institution and FinTech labels should remain consistent.

## 35. Unit Awareness
Crore, lakh, percentage and count must not be mixed without conversion.

## 36. Financial Data
Real financial datasets can contain sensitive information.

## 37. Privacy
Customer and transaction data should be protected in real projects.

## 38. Security
Financial systems require appropriate data-security controls.

## 40. Final Learning
Finance and FinTech provide multiple business-data dimensions for structured analysis.
