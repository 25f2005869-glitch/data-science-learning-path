# 📊 Day 072 — Introduction to Finance Industry and FinTech

## 📌 Topic
Introduction to the finance industry and technology-enabled financial services.

## 🎯 Objectives
1. Understand the broad finance industry structure.
2. Identify major institution types.
3. Understand the role of banks.
4. Understand NBFCs.
5. Understand insurance services.
6. Understand capital markets.
7. Understand payments.
8. Define FinTech.
9. Identify major FinTech categories.
10. Analyse finance-related business data.

## 🏦 Finance Industry
Banking.
NBFCs.
Insurance.
Capital markets.
Payments.
Investment services.

## 💻 FinTech
FinTech refers to technology-enabled financial services and processes.
It can include digital payments, digital lending, WealthTech, InsurTech and RegTech.

## 📊 Dataset
The workbook contains 30 illustrative finance entities.
The dataset is synthetic and designed for learning.
Institution types are represented across the dataset.
FinTech categories are included as an analytical dimension.
Regions and financial services are also recorded.

## 📏 Core Measures
Entities.
Employees.
Annual Revenue.
Active Customers.
Digital Transactions.
Transaction Value.
Digital Adoption.
Revenue per Employee.
Transactions per Customer.

## 🧮 Key Calculations
Revenue per Employee = Revenue × 100 / Employees.
Transactions per Customer = Digital Transactions × 100000 / Active Customers.

## 🔍 Analysis Dimensions
Institution Type.
FinTech Category.
Region.
Primary Service.
