# 📊 Day 082 — A/B Testing Presentation

## 🎯 Objective
- Convert the Day 081 A/B test into a business presentation.
- Structure the experiment around a clear business question.
- Present the control and treatment groups.
- Present conversion, click rate and lift.
- Add nudge, segment and region context.

## 🧩 Case Context
- The case uses a synthetic PayLater experiment.
- Control is the reference experience.
- Treatment represents a changed nudge experience.
- The dataset is educational and illustrative.
- It is not official IIT Madras data.

## 🧪 Presentation Structure
1. Business Question
2. Experiment Design
3. Dataset & Sample
4. Control vs Treatment
5. Nudge-Level Analysis
6. Segment & Region Analysis
7. Business Interpretation
8. Limitations & Next Steps

## 📈 Primary Metric
- PayLater conversion is the primary outcome.
- Exposure is the denominator.
- Conversion = PayLater users / exposures × 100.

## 🧮 Lift
- Absolute lift = Treatment rate − Control rate.
- Absolute lift is reported in percentage points.
- Relative lift = Absolute lift / Control rate × 100.

## 📊 Dashboard
- Control versus Treatment conversion.

## ⚠️ Interpretation
- Descriptive differences should be stated separately from causal explanations.
- Appropriate randomisation and experimental assumptions matter for causal interpretation.
- Small samples can produce unstable subgroup rates.
- Synthetic results cannot be generalised to real PayLater users.

## 🧠 Key Takeaways
- A/B testing presentation should begin with a measurable question.
- Control and Treatment must be defined before showing results.
- Conversion needs a clear exposure denominator.
- Absolute lift is reported in percentage points.
- Relative lift is reported as a percentage change.
- Nudge, segment and region views are supporting analyses.
- Sample counts should accompany rates.
- Causal language depends on experimental design.
- Synthetic results are for learning and portfolio practice.
- The final slide should state limitations and next steps.
