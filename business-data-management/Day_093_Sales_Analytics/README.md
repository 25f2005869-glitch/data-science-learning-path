## 💰 4. Revenue and Profit Analysis

### Revenue

Revenue represents the sales value generated from orders.

**Formula:**

Revenue = Quantity × Unit Price

### Profit

Profit represents the amount remaining after subtracting cost from revenue.

**Formula:**

Profit = Revenue − Cost

### Profit Margin

Profit Margin shows profit as a percentage of revenue.

**Formula:**

Profit Margin (%) = (Profit ÷ Revenue) × 100

---

## 🛒 5. Average Order Value

Average Order Value (AOV) represents the average revenue generated per order.

**Formula:**

AOV = Total Revenue ÷ Number of Orders

AOV can be used to understand the average value of customer orders.
