## 8. Causal Inference
- Random assignment helps establish comparable groups in expectation.
- A treatment-control difference can support causal interpretation when design assumptions hold.
- Non-randomised comparisons can contain confounding.
- Descriptive segment differences should not automatically be interpreted as treatment effects.

## 9. PayLater-Specific Analysis
- Conversion measures usage.
- Purchase value measures monetary activity.
- Repayment measures a later-stage outcome.
- These outcomes should not be merged into one unexplained score.
- The business question determines the primary metric.

## 10. Sample Size
