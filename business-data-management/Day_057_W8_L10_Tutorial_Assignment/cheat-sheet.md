## 🧮 Inventory Formula

Closing Inventory:

Opening + Purchases − Usage

## 💰 Value Formula

Inventory Value:

Closing Inventory × Unit Cost

## 📅 Daily Usage

Material Usage ÷ 30

## ⏱️ Lead-Time Demand

Average Daily Usage × Lead Time

## 🛡️ Safety Stock

Average Daily Usage × Safety Stock Days

## 📌 Reorder Point

Lead-Time Demand + Safety Stock

## 📉 Inventory Gap

Closing Inventory − Reorder Point

## 🚦 Status

Gap < 0

→ Below Reorder Point

Gap ≥ 0

→ At/Above Reorder Point
