## 🧮 Core Calculations

Closing Inventory:

Opening Inventory + Purchases − Material Usage

Inventory Value:

Closing Inventory × Unit Cost

Average Daily Usage:

Material Usage ÷ 30

Lead-Time Demand:

Average Daily Usage × Lead Time

Safety Stock:

Average Daily Usage × Safety Stock Days

Reorder Point:

Lead-Time Demand + Safety Stock

Inventory Gap:

Closing Inventory − Reorder Point
