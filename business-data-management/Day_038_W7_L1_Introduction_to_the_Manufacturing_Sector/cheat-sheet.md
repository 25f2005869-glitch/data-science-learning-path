# ⚡ Day 038 Cheat Sheet — Manufacturing Sector

**Author:** Saloni Tiwari  
**Programme:** IIT Madras BS Degree — Diploma Level  
**Day:** 038  
**Topic:** Introduction to the Manufacturing Sector

## 🏭 Core Terms

Manufacturing → production of physical goods

Production → units produced

Sales → units sold

Scrap → units classified as production loss

Revenue → sales value

Production Cost → production expenditure

## 🧮 Formulas

Gross Margin = Revenue − Production Cost

Scrap Rate = Scrap Units ÷ Production Units

## 📋 Analysis Workflow

1. Understand the business context.
2. Inspect the dataset.
3. Check field definitions.
4. Calculate KPIs.
5. Group by product.
6. Group by region.
7. Group by customer type.
8. Visualize results.
9. Write insights.

## 🛠️ Excel

SUMIF can create grouped summaries.

Pivot Tables can summarize manufacturing data.

Charts can communicate comparisons.

## 🐍 Python

groupby() creates grouped summaries.

agg() calculates multiple metrics.

matplotlib creates charts.

## ⚠️ Remember

Do not confuse production units with sales units.

Do not treat scrap as sales.

Keep revenue and cost separate.

Validate formulas before interpretation.
