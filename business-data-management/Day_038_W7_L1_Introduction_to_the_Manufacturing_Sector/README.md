## 🚀 Day Overview

This day introduces manufacturing-sector data from a business analytics perspective.

The practical dataset connects production, sales, scrap, revenue and production cost.

The objective is to understand how operational data becomes business information.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand manufacturing data.
- Identify production metrics.
- Compare production and sales.
- Understand scrap as a quality metric.
- Analyze revenue and production cost.
- Compare products, regions and customer types.

## 🏭 Manufacturing Data

Manufacturing converts inputs into physical products through production processes.

Business data can describe production volume, sales volume, quality losses and financial performance.

## 📊 Main Metrics

- Production Units
- Sales Units
- Scrap Units
- Revenue
- Production Cost
- Gross Margin
- Scrap Rate

## 🧮 Important Formulas

Gross Margin = Revenue − Production Cost

Scrap Rate = Scrap Units / Production Units
