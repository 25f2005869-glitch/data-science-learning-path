# 📊 Day 069 — Recruitment Process and Onboarding

## 📌 Topic

Day 069 studies recruitment as a structured business process and then connects the selected-candidate stage with onboarding execution.

## 🎯 Objectives

1. Understand the recruitment lifecycle.
2. Separate sourcing from screening.
3. Track assessment and selection.
4. Measure recruitment duration.
5. Analyse recruitment cost.
6. Compare recruitment channels.
7. Compare departments.
8. Review monthly recruitment activity.
9. Understand onboarding tasks.
10. Track onboarding completion and access readiness.

## 🔄 Recruitment Process

Requirement identification.

Role and job-description definition.

Candidate sourcing.

Application collection.

Initial screening.

Assessment.

Interview.

Selection.

Offer.

Pre-joining documentation.

Joining.

Onboarding.

## 🧩 Dataset

The workbook contains 30 illustrative recruitment records.

The period covered is January to March 2023.

Departments include Technology, Finance, HR, Sales and Operations.

Regions include North, South and West.

Recruitment channels include Job Portal, Employee Referral, Internal Transfer and Recruitment Agency.

## 📏 Core Recruitment Metrics

Candidate count.

Application count.

Selected candidates.

Selection rate.

Average days to fill.

Average recruitment cost.

Total recruitment cost.

Average assessment score.

Cost per selected candidate.

## 🧮 Important Formulas

Selection Rate = Selected Candidates / Total Candidates × 100.

Cost per Day = Recruitment Cost / Days to Fill.

Cost per Selected = Total Recruitment Cost / Selected Candidates.

## 🏠 Onboarding

The onboarding dataset tracks selected candidates after recruitment.

Tasks are divided into pre-joining and joining activities.

Examples include documentation, background verification, orientation, manager introduction and IT/access setup.

## ⚠️ Important

The dataset is synthetic.

It is designed for BDM practice.

The metrics are illustrative.

Real recruitment systems require additional fields and controls.

Recruitment analytics should be interpreted within the organisation's documented policies.

## 🐍 Python

The Python script reads the workbook using pandas.

It creates grouped recruitment summaries.

It calculates selection rates and cost metrics.

It analyses onboarding task completion.

It exports CSV files.

It generates four charts.

## 🧠 Key Takeaway

Recruitment analytics becomes more useful when process volume, time, cost and outcomes are studied together.

Onboarding should then be tracked as a separate execution process with clear task status and ownership.
