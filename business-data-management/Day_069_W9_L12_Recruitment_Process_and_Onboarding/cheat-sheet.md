## Formulas

Selection Rate = Selected / Candidates × 100.

Cost per Day = Recruitment Cost / Days to Fill.

Cost per Selected = Total Recruitment Cost / Selected.

Onboarding Completion Rate = Completed Tasks / Total Tasks × 100.

High volume = more recruitment activity.

Selection rate = conversion measure.

Days to fill = process-duration measure.

Recruitment cost = resource measure.

Assessment score = evaluation measure.

Completion rate = onboarding execution measure.
