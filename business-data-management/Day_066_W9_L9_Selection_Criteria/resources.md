## Responsible Use

A selection model should be transparent about its assumptions.

Illustrative scores should not be treated as real hiring evidence.

A numeric score should not automatically replace contextual review.

Criteria should be relevant to the role being evaluated.

Sensitive candidate information should be handled appropriately.
