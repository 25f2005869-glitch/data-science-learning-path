# ⚡ Day 059 Cheat Sheet — Tech Enterprises

## Tech Enterprise

Technology enterprise = business substantially relying on technology.

Sector = technology/business category.

Region = geographic grouping.

Business model = B2B or B2C.

## Common Technology Sectors

SaaS

AI Services

Software Development

Data Analytics

FinTech

EdTech

HealthTech

RetailTech

## B2B vs B2C

B2B → business customer.

B2C → individual consumer.

B2B often involves organizational buying processes.

B2C often involves consumer-oriented sales and service.

## Core Metrics

Enterprise Count = number of enterprise records.

Total Employees = sum of employee counts.

Total Revenue = sum of annual revenue.

Average Revenue = total revenue / enterprises.

Total Active Clients = sum of active clients.

Total New Clients = sum of new clients.

Average Satisfaction = mean satisfaction score.

## Interpretation Rules

Use consistent units.

Define the population.

Define the time period.

Check data quality.

Separate description from explanation.

Do not infer causation from simple group differences.

## Enterprise Scale

Employees = workforce indicator.

Revenue = financial activity indicator.

Clients = customer activity indicator.

Satisfaction = customer-experience indicator.

No single metric describes every aspect of an enterprise.

## Quick Revision

Technology enterprise = technology-focused business.

B2B = business-to-business.

B2C = business-to-consumer.

Sector = business/technology category.

Revenue = financial activity measure.

Employees = workforce measure.

Clients = customer activity measure.

## Data Quality

Check IDs.

Check duplicates.

Check missing values.

Check categories.

Check numeric ranges.

Check units.

Check definitions.

## Responsible Analytics

Protect sensitive information.

Use aggregated data when appropriate.

Document definitions.

Avoid unsupported causal claims.

Keep illustrative data separate from real business evidence.
