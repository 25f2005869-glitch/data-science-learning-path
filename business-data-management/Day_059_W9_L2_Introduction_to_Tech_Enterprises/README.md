# 💻 Day 059 — Introduction to Tech Enterprises

## 📌 Day Overview

Day 059 — W9_L2 introduces the idea of **technology enterprises** and their business characteristics.

This package uses an illustrative dataset to practice enterprise-level business analysis.

The data is educational and is not official IIT Madras or company data.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

1. Understand what a technology enterprise is.
2. Recognize different technology sectors.
3. Distinguish B2B and B2C business models.
4. Compare enterprise scale using employees, revenue and clients.
5. Build sector and regional summaries.
6. Practice business interpretation using structured data.

## 💻 Technology Enterprises

A technology enterprise is a business whose products or services rely substantially on technology.

Technology businesses can operate in software, cloud services, artificial intelligence, financial technology, education technology, health technology and analytics.

Different technology sectors can have different operating models and customer structures.

## 🏢 Dataset

The workbook contains 24 illustrative technology enterprises.

Fields include enterprise ID, enterprise name, technology sector, region, business model, employees, annual revenue, active clients, new clients and client satisfaction.

The dataset contains both B2B and B2C enterprises.

## 📊 Core Metrics

Enterprise count describes the number of enterprises in the dataset.

Employees provide one descriptive measure of enterprise scale.

Annual revenue provides a financial activity measure.

Active clients describe current customer activity.

New clients describe recorded customer additions.

Client satisfaction provides a simple customer-experience indicator.

## ⚠️ Interpretation

Revenue differences do not automatically explain business success.

Employee count alone does not describe every dimension of enterprise scale.

Client satisfaction should be interpreted using its measurement definition.

Small illustrative datasets should not be generalized to the technology industry.
