# 📝 Day 039 Notes — ACE Gears Case Introduction

**Author:** Saloni Tiwari  
**Programme:** IIT Madras BS Degree — Diploma Level  
**Day:** 039  
**Topic:** ACE Gears Case Introduction

## 1. Case Introduction

This day introduces the ACE Gears manufacturing case.

The purpose is to understand the case context and dataset before deeper analysis.

## 2. Dataset Structure

Each record contains product, region and customer information along with operational and financial measures.

## 3. Product

The sample contains Gears, Shafts and Bearings.

Product is a major business dimension.

## 4. Region

The sample contains North, South and West.

Region allows geographic performance comparisons.

## 5. Customer Type

The sample contains OEM and Distributor customers.

Customer type provides another analytical dimension.

## 6. Production Units

Production Units describe recorded manufacturing output.

## 7. Sales Units

Sales Units describe recorded units sold.

Comparing production and sales can reveal differences in operational and commercial activity.

## 8. Scrap Units

Scrap Units represent units classified as scrap in the sample.

## 9. Scrap Rate

Scrap Rate = Scrap Units / Production Units

This provides a simple normalized quality-related measure.

## 10. Revenue

Revenue represents recorded sales value.

It can be grouped by product, region or customer type.

## 11. Production Cost

Production Cost represents recorded production expenditure.

## 12. Gross Margin

Gross Margin = Revenue − Production Cost

This provides a simple difference between recorded revenue and production cost.

## 13. Product Summary

The Product_Summary sheet aggregates major operational and financial measures.

## 14. Regional Summary

Region_Summary provides the same analytical framework by region.

## 15. Customer Summary

Customer_Summary compares OEM and Distributor groups.

## 16. Case Questions

The Case_Questions sheet converts the dataset into business-analysis questions.

## 17. Next Analysis

The prepared dataset can support deeper product, portfolio, regional and operational analysis in subsequent case stages.
