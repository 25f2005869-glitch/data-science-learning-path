## 🎓 Learning Context

This day introduces the ACE Gears manufacturing case used for BDM analytical practice.

The focus is understanding the case and preparing the data for deeper analysis.

Product_Summary provides product aggregation.

Customer_Summary provides customer-type aggregation.

Case_Questions contains business questions.

Data_Dictionary explains fields.

Business_Insights provides learning observations.

Instructions provides the workflow.
