## 🧮 Excel Functions

### SUM

`=SUM(G2:K2)`

Adds multiple spending categories.

---

### AVERAGEIF

`=AVERAGEIF(Location_Range,"Rural",Spending_Range)`

Finds the average for rural respondents.

---

### AVERAGEIFS

`=AVERAGEIFS(Spending_Range,Location_Range,"Rural",Age_Range,"18-25")`

Finds the average for rural respondents belonging to a particular age group.

---

### COUNTIF

`=COUNTIF(Location_Range,"Rural")`

Counts rural respondents.

---

### COUNTIFS

`=COUNTIFS(Location_Range,"Rural",Age_Range,"26-35")`

Counts rural respondents in a particular age group.
