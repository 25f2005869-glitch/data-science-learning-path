# Day 017 Practice — Loan Data & Pivot Tables

## Practice Set A — Pivot Basics

1. Create a PivotTable showing Total Loans by Company.

2. Sort companies from highest to lowest loan volume.

3. Add Total Customers to the same summary.

4. Create a separate PivotTable showing Total Loans by Region.

5. Create a third PivotTable showing Total Loans by Loan Type.

## Practice Set B — Market Share

6. Calculate the total market loan volume.

7. Calculate market share for each company.

8. Display market share as a percentage.

9. Check that company shares total approximately 100%.

10. Identify the largest measured market participant.

## Practice Set C — Cross Analysis

11. Create Company in Rows and Region in Columns.

12. Put Loans in Values.

13. Identify the largest company-region cell.

14. Compare Alpha Finance across the four regions.

15. Compare the same region across all four companies.

## Practice Set D — Formula Validation

16. Use SUMIF to validate Alpha Finance's total loans.

17. Use SUMIF to validate North region loans.

18. Use SUMIF to validate Personal loan volume.

19. Use SUMIFS to validate one company-region combination.

20. Compare the formula result with the pivot result.

## Practice Set E — Visualization

21. Create a column chart for company loan volume.

22. Create a column chart for regional loan volume.

23. Create a composition chart for company market share.

24. Add clear titles and axis labels.

25. Write two observations below the charts.

## Business Questions

Which company leads the measured loan market?

Which region has the largest total loan volume?

Which loan type contributes the largest volume?

Does the leading company lead every region?

Which company-region combination has the lowest loan volume?

### Practical checkpoint

- Identify the business question before creating a summary.
- Select the correct measure for the question.
- Check totals before interpreting percentages.
- Compare company, region, and loan-type patterns.
- Use a pivot table to reduce manual aggregation.
- Validate a pivot result with a formula when useful.
- Record one evidence-based business insight.
- Save the workbook with a clear day-wise filename.
- Keep raw data separate from analysis outputs.
- Use consistent field names throughout the workbook.

### Revision checklist

- Market share is based on a defined market measure.
- Loan volume can be summarized by company.
- Loan volume can be summarized by region.
- Loan volume can be summarized by loan type.
- Pivot tables support grouping and aggregation.
- SUMIF can reproduce a one-dimensional summary.
- SUMIFS can reproduce a multi-condition summary.
- Percentage of grand total can express share.
- Charts should answer a specific business question.
- A chart is not a substitute for interpretation.
