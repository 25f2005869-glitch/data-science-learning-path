---

## 🔹 2. Demand

**Demand** represents the quantity of a product consumers are willing to buy at a given price.

Generally:

**Price ↑ → Demand ↓**

**Price ↓ → Demand ↑**

---

## 🔹 3. Supply

**Supply** represents the quantity producers are willing to offer at a given price.

Generally:

**Price ↑ → Supply ↑**

**Price ↓ → Supply ↓**

---

## 🔹 4. Demand–Supply Analysis

We can compare Demand and Supply using Excel.

**Demand > Supply** → shortage situation

**Supply > Demand** → surplus situation

A simple Excel calculation:

`Demand - Supply`

This gives the **Demand–Supply Gap**.

## 🔹 5. Useful Excel Functions

| Function | Use |
|---|---|
| `SUM` | Total |
| `AVERAGE` | Average |
| `MIN` | Lowest value |
| `MAX` | Highest value |

Example:

`=SUM(C2:C13)`

`=AVERAGE(B2:B13)`
