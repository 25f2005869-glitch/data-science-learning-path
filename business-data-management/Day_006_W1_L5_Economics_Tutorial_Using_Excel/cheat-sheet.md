## 💰 Economics Basics

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Demand | Quantity consumers are willing to buy |
| Supply | Quantity producers are willing to offer |
| Price | Amount paid for a product |
| Consumer Income | Income available to consumers |
| Producer Cost | Cost incurred by producers |

---

## 📈 Demand

**Price ↑ → Demand generally ↓**

**Price ↓ → Demand generally ↑**

---

## 📉 Supply

**Price ↑ → Supply generally ↑**

**Price ↓ → Supply generally ↓**

---

## ⚖️ Demand–Supply Gap

**Demand − Supply = Gap**

- Demand > Supply → Shortage
- Supply > Demand → Surplus

---

## 📊 Excel Functions

| Function | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `SUM` | Total | `=SUM(C2:C13)` |
| `AVERAGE` | Average | `=AVERAGE(B2:B13)` |
| `MIN` | Lowest value | `=MIN(C2:C13)` |
| `MAX` | Highest value | `=MAX(C2:C13)` |
