## 💼 Business Case

A product has a high DSI.

Do not immediately call it inefficient.

Review:

- Sales velocity
- Inventory
- Observation period
- Demand pattern
- Replenishment context

## 💼 Business Case 02

A product has high sales.

Check whether its inventory position supports the sales activity.

## 💼 Business Case 03

A month has high purchases.

Check sales and closing stock before interpreting the movement.
