## 📐 DSI Resource

Formula:

`Average Inventory / Average Daily Sales`

Unit:

`Days`

## 🧮 Sales Velocity

Formula:

`Total Sales Units / Observation Days`

Unit:

`Units per day`

## 🔄 Turnover Resource

Formula:

`Total Sales Units / Average Inventory`

Unit:

`Times`
