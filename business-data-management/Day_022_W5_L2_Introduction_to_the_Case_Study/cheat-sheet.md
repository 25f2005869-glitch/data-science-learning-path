## 🎯 Core Idea

## 🏢 Case Study

11. Case Study = Structured business problem.
12. Objective = What the analysis needs to achieve.
13. Dataset = Collection of business observations.
14. Observation = Usually one row of data.
15. Variable = A column describing an observation.
16. Metric = A measurable business quantity.
17. Dimension = A field used to group or compare data.
18. KPI = Key Performance Indicator.
19. Insight = Meaningful interpretation of data.
20. Decision = Business action supported by analysis.
