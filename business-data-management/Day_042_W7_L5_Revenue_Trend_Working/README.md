## 🔎 Main Analysis
The central analysis is monthly revenue.
The workbook calculates total revenue for each month.
MoM growth compares the current month with the previous month.
Product and region summaries provide supporting business views.

## 📈 Visualization
The dashboard contains a monthly revenue line chart.
It also contains product and regional revenue charts.
Charts use explicit formatting so the workbook is presentation-ready.

## 🧠 Business Interpretation
A rising revenue line indicates increasing revenue over the observed period.
A declining section can indicate a slowdown that needs investigation.
Revenue should be interpreted together with sales units and production.
Product and regional contributions help identify where revenue is concentrated.

## ⚠️ Dataset Note
The dataset in this practical workbook is illustrative.
It is not official IIT Madras course data.
