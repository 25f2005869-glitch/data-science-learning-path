# ⚡ Day 051 Cheat Sheet — Profitability & Reordering

## 💰 Profitability
Revenue = Sales Units × Selling Price.
Material Cost = Sales Units × Material Cost per Unit.
Labour Cost = Sales Units × Labour Cost per Unit.
Overhead Cost = Sales Units × Overhead per Unit.
Total Cost = Material + Labour + Overhead.
Gross Profit = Revenue − Total Cost.
Unit Profit = Gross Profit / Sales Units.
Unit Margin = Gross Profit / Revenue.

## 📦 Reordering
Lead-Time Demand = Average Daily Usage × Lead Time.
Reorder Point = Lead-Time Demand + Safety Stock.
Reorder Now when:
Closing Stock ≤ Reorder Point.

## 📊 Important Metrics
Revenue → sales value.
Total Cost → total cost.
Gross Profit → revenue minus cost.
Unit Profit → profit per unit.
Margin → profit percentage.
Lead-Time Demand → expected usage during lead time.
Safety Stock → buffer.
Reorder Point → replenishment trigger.

## 🧠 Interpretation
Unit profit is currency per unit.
Margin is a percentage.
Reorder point is a stock-level planning metric.
Safety stock is added to lead-time demand.

## ⚠️ Remember
Do not confuse revenue with profit.
Do not confuse unit profit with margin.
Do not confuse lead-time demand with safety stock.
Do not treat illustrative data as real business data.

## 🎯 Analysis Order
Sales → Revenue → Costs → Profit → Margin.
Usage → Lead Time → Safety Stock → Reorder Point.
