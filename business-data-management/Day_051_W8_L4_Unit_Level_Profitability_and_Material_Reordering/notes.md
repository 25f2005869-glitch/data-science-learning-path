# 📝 Day 051 Notes — Unit Level Profitability and Material Reordering

## 1. Introduction
Unit-level profitability examines economics at the product-unit level.
It connects selling price with unit cost and profit.

## 2. Revenue
Revenue represents the sales value generated by units sold.
Revenue = Sales Units × Selling Price per Unit.

## 3. Material Cost
Material cost is calculated from units sold and material cost per unit.

## 4. Labour Cost
Labour cost represents direct labour expenditure associated with the units.

## 5. Overhead
Overhead is an illustrative operating cost allocated per unit.

## 6. Total Cost
Total cost combines material, labour and overhead costs.
Total Cost = Material Cost + Labour Cost + Overhead Cost.

## 7. Gross Profit
Gross profit is revenue minus total cost.
Gross Profit = Revenue − Total Cost.

## 8. Unit Profit
Unit profit shows profit generated per sold unit.
Unit Profit = Gross Profit / Sales Units.

## 9. Unit Margin
Unit margin expresses gross profit as a percentage of revenue.
Unit Margin = Gross Profit / Revenue.

## 10. Material Demand
Material demand represents the material quantity required for production or sales activity.

## 11. Average Daily Usage
Average daily material usage represents expected daily material consumption.

## 12. Lead Time
Lead time is the time between placing an order and receiving the material.

## 13. Lead-Time Demand
Lead-time demand estimates material use during supplier lead time.
Lead-Time Demand = Average Daily Usage × Lead Time.

## 14. Safety Stock
Safety stock is an additional inventory buffer.
It is used to provide protection against uncertainty in this practice model.

## 15. Reorder Point
Reorder point is the inventory level at which replenishment planning is triggered.
Reorder Point = Lead-Time Demand + Safety Stock.

## 16. Reorder Status
The workbook uses a simple rule.
If closing stock is at or below reorder point, status is Reorder Now.
Otherwise the status is Monitor.

## 17. Product Analysis
Unit_Profitability compares product economics.
It includes revenue, cost, gross profit and margin.

## 18. Material Analysis
Material_Reorder_Analysis compares usage, lead time, safety stock and reorder point.

## 19. Monthly Analysis
Monthly_Profitability provides a time-based profitability view.

## 20. Regional Analysis
Regional_Profitability compares revenue, cost and profit by region.

## 23. Interpretation
Unit profit and margin answer different questions.
Unit profit is expressed in currency per unit.
Margin is expressed as a percentage.

## 24. Business Discussion
Profitability analysis can support product-level discussion.
Reorder analysis can support material planning.
Both views can be considered together.
