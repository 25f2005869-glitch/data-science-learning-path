## 💰 Profitability Concepts
Revenue is sales units multiplied by selling price.
Total cost includes material, labour and overhead.
Gross profit is revenue minus total cost.
Unit profit is gross profit divided by sales units.
Unit margin is gross profit divided by revenue.

## 📦 Material Reordering
Material planning uses average daily usage.
Lead-time demand represents expected usage during supplier lead time.
Safety stock provides a buffer.
Reorder point combines lead-time demand and safety stock.

## 📐 Main Formulas
Revenue = Sales Units × Selling Price per Unit.
Total Cost = Material Cost + Labour Cost + Overhead Cost.
Gross Profit = Revenue − Total Cost.
Unit Profit = Gross Profit / Sales Units.
Unit Margin = Gross Profit / Revenue.
Lead-Time Demand = Average Daily Usage × Lead Time.
Reorder Point = Lead-Time Demand + Safety Stock.
