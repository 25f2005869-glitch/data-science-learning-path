## 10. Important Relationships

Production = Good Units + Scrap Units.

Good Units = Production - Scrap.

Scrap Rate = Scrap / Production.

## 11. Final Revision

Production → Scrap → Good Units → Scrap Rate → Cost → Product → Month → Region
