# 📘 Day 074 — PayBuddy Case: Credit Product Introduction

## 🎯 Objective

- Understand credit-product data.
- Analyse product, region and segment dimensions.
- Build descriptive KPIs.

## 💳 Products

- Credit Line.
- Merchant Credit.
- Working Capital.

## 📊 Measures

- Credit limit.
- Credit utilised.
- Utilisation percentage.
- Available credit.
- Transactions.
- Repayment score.
- Customer activity score.

## 🧮 Derived Metrics

- Utilisation = utilised / limit × 100.
- Available credit = limit − utilised.
- Activity index = transactions × activity score / 100.

- Product mix doughnut.

## 🔎 Questions

## ⚠️ Interpretation

- Use descriptive language.
- Check units.
- Check denominators.
- Do not infer real creditworthiness.
- Do not claim causation.
