**Topic:** Unit Level Profitability Working

## 📖 Concept Overview

Day 052 focuses on detailed unit-level profitability working.

The workflow connects sales volume, selling price, revenue, cost components, gross profit, unit cost, unit profit and margin.

The workbook uses an illustrative ACE Gears-style manufacturing dataset created for practice.

This is not official IIT Madras data and should not be interpreted as actual company performance.

## 1️⃣ Sales Units

Sales Units represent the quantity sold.

They are the volume base for the unit-level calculations.

## 2️⃣ Selling Price per Unit

This is the modeled selling price attached to one unit.

It is multiplied by sales units to calculate revenue.

## 3️⃣ Revenue

Revenue represents the modeled sales value.

Revenue is calculated before subtracting the modeled costs.

## 4️⃣ Material Cost

Material cost represents the modeled material expenditure associated with the sold quantity.

The calculation uses material cost per unit.

## 5️⃣ Labour Cost

Labour cost represents the modeled labour expenditure associated with the sold quantity.

The calculation uses labour cost per unit.

## 6️⃣ Overhead Cost

Overhead represents the modeled indirect operating cost allocated per unit in this exercise.

## 7️⃣ Total Cost

Total Cost combines material, labour and overhead.

`Total Cost = Material Cost + Labour Cost + Overhead Cost`

## 8️⃣ Unit Cost

Unit Cost converts total modeled cost into a per-unit measure.

`Unit Cost = Total Cost ÷ Sales Units`

## 9️⃣ Gross Profit

Gross Profit is revenue less the modeled total cost.

`Gross Profit = Revenue − Total Cost`

## 🔟 Unit Profit

Unit Profit expresses gross profit for one unit.

`Unit Profit = Gross Profit ÷ Sales Units`

## 📌 Margin Percentage

Margin expresses gross profit relative to revenue.

`Margin % = Gross Profit ÷ Revenue`

## 📦 Product-Level Working

Product-level grouping helps compare Gears, Shafts and Bearings using consistent metrics.

## 📅 Monthly Working

Monthly grouping helps inspect changes in volume, revenue, cost and profitability over time.

## 🌍 Regional Working

Regional grouping provides a separate view of North, South and West.

## 🧩 Cost Components

Material, labour and overhead should first be inspected separately.

Combining them gives total modeled cost.

## ⚠️ Interpretation Caution

Illustrative numbers are useful for learning formulas and analytical workflow.

They are not evidence of actual ACE Gears or IIT Madras business performance.
