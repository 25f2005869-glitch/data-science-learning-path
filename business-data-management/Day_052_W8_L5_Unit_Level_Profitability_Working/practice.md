Check the calculation level.

## 🔎 Review Checklist

- Check the input values before calculating.
- Trace the formula from units to revenue.
- Trace each cost component separately.
- Calculate total cost before profit.
- Convert total cost into unit cost.
- Calculate gross profit from revenue and total cost.
- Calculate unit profit from gross profit and units.
- Calculate margin as a percentage of revenue.
- Compare products using the same metric.
- Compare months using the same metric.
- Review regional differences carefully.
- Use the dashboard as a visual summary.
- Validate unusual results against the raw data.
- Do not treat illustrative data as company performance.
- Record the business interpretation after the calculation.
