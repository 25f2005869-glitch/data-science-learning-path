# Day 052 — Profitability Cheat Sheet

**Author:** Saloni Tiwari  
**Programme:** IIT Madras BS Degree — Diploma Level  
**Day:** 052  
**Topic:** Unit Level Profitability Working

## ⚡ Quick Formulas

`Revenue = Units × Price`

`Material Cost = Units × Material Cost/Unit`

`Labour Cost = Units × Labour Cost/Unit`

`Overhead Cost = Units × Overhead/Unit`

`Total Cost = Material + Labour + Overhead`

`Unit Cost = Total Cost ÷ Units`

`Gross Profit = Revenue − Total Cost`

`Unit Profit = Gross Profit ÷ Units`

`Margin % = Gross Profit ÷ Revenue`

## 🔢 Correct Calculation Order

1. Sales Units
2. Price per Unit
3. Revenue
4. Material Cost
5. Labour Cost
6. Overhead Cost
7. Total Cost
8. Unit Cost
9. Gross Profit
10. Unit Profit
11. Margin %

## 📊 Metrics at a Glance

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Revenue | Modeled sales value |
| Total Cost | Modeled combined cost |
| Gross Profit | Revenue minus total cost |
| Unit Cost | Cost per sold unit |
| Unit Profit | Gross profit per sold unit |
| Margin % | Gross profit as share of revenue |

## 🧠 Interpretation

Use Unit Cost for cost per unit.

Use Unit Profit for profit per unit.

Use Gross Profit for total modeled profit.

Use Margin % for relative profitability.

## 🏭 Product Analysis

Compare products using the same profitability metric.

Do not compare one product's total profit with another product's unit profit.

Keep the analysis level consistent.

## 📅 Monthly Analysis

Monthly analysis can show changes in revenue and profitability.

Higher sales volume can affect total revenue and total profit.

## 🌍 Regional Analysis

Regional analysis groups records by region.

It can be used to inspect differences in sales and modeled profitability.

## 💰 Cost Analysis

Separate material, labour and overhead before calculating total cost.

This helps explain the composition of total cost.
