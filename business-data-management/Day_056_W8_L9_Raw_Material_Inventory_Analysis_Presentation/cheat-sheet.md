## 🧮 Formula 1 — Closing Inventory

Opening Inventory + Purchases − Material Usage

## 🧮 Formula 2 — Inventory Value

Closing Inventory × Unit Cost

## 🧮 Formula 3 — Average Daily Usage

Material Usage ÷ Number of Days

## 🧮 Formula 4 — Lead-Time Demand

Average Daily Usage × Lead Time

## 🧮 Formula 5 — Reorder Point

Lead-Time Demand + Safety Stock

## 🧮 Formula 6 — Inventory Gap

Closing Inventory − Reorder Point

## 🚦 Status Logic

If Inventory Gap < 0:

Below Reorder Point

If Inventory Gap ≥ 0:

At/Above Reorder Point
