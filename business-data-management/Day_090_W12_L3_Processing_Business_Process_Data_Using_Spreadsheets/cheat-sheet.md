## 🧠 1. Core Workflow

**Raw Data → Organise → Calculate → Summarise → Visualise → Interpret**

---

## 📊 2. Business Process Data

Business process data records activities performed during business operations.

### Common Fields

- Date
- Business Function
- Process
- Orders
- Revenue
- Cost
- Delivery Days
- On-Time Deliveries
- Deliveries
- Returned Orders
- Customer Satisfaction

---

## 🔢 3. Important Calculations

### Profit

**Profit = Revenue − Cost**

### Average Order Value

**AOV = Revenue / Orders**

### Cost per Order

**Cost per Order = Cost / Orders**

### On-Time Delivery Rate

**On-Time Delivery Rate = On-Time Deliveries / Deliveries × 100**

### Return Rate

**Return Rate = Returned Orders / Orders × 100**

### Profit Margin

**Profit Margin = Profit / Revenue × 100**

## 🧮 4. Useful Spreadsheet Functions

| Function | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `SUM()` | Adds values |
| `AVERAGE()` | Calculates average |
| `COUNT()` | Counts numbers |
| `COUNTA()` | Counts non-empty cells |
| `MIN()` | Finds minimum value |
| `MAX()` | Finds maximum value |
| `IF()` | Applies a condition |
| `COUNTIF()` | Counts values matching a condition |
| `SUMIF()` | Adds values matching a condition |
| `AVERAGEIF()` | Calculates conditional average |

---

## 🔍 5. Sorting

Sorting arranges data in a specific order.

### Examples

- Revenue → Largest to Smallest
- Cost → Smallest to Largest
- Date → Oldest to Newest
- Customer Satisfaction → Highest to Lowest

---

## 🎯 6. Filtering

Filtering displays only records that match selected conditions.

### Examples

- Business Function = Sales
- Process = Delivery
- Revenue > 100000
- Customer Satisfaction > 80%

**Important:** Filtering does not delete the other records.

---

## 📊 11. KPI Interpretation

### High Profit

May indicate stronger revenue relative to cost.

### High Cost per Order

May indicate higher processing or operational cost.

### High Return Rate

Shows that a larger proportion of orders were returned.

### High On-Time Delivery Rate

Shows stronger delivery performance in the measured data.

### High Customer Satisfaction

Indicates higher reported satisfaction in the dataset.

> KPI patterns alone do not establish the cause of a business result.

---

## ⚠️ 13. Common Mistakes

- Using incorrect formulas
- Dividing by zero
- Mixing percentages and decimals
- Sorting only one column instead of the complete table
- Applying filters incorrectly
- Using inconsistent date formats
- Leaving duplicate records unnoticed
- Creating charts without checking the data
- Drawing conclusions without reviewing the underlying data

## 💡 15. Quick Business Questions

## 🧠 16. Key Takeaway

**Organise → Calculate → Summarise → Visualise → Interpret**

The goal of spreadsheet processing is not only to calculate numbers, but also to convert business process data into useful business information.

## 📚 Summary

- Business process data records operational activities.
- Spreadsheets can organise, process, and analyse this data.

- Charts make patterns easier to understand.
- KPIs help measure business performance.
