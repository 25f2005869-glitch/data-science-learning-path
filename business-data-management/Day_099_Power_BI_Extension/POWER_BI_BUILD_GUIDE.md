## Current Starting Point
The Excel workbook is imported into Power BI Desktop.

Selected tables:
- SalesData
- Customer Dimension (Dim_Customer)
- Product Dimension (Dim_Product)
- Date Dimension (Dim_Date)

## Build Sequence

### 1. DAX Measures
Create Total Revenue, Total Cost, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, Total Orders, Total Quantity, Average Order Value and Customers.

### 2. KPI Cards
Create four cards:
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin %

### 3. Basic Charts
Create:
- Revenue by Category — Column
- Revenue by Region — Bar
- Monthly Revenue — Line
- Revenue by Segment — Donut

### 4. Advanced Charts
Create:
- Treemap
- Scatter
- Combo
- Waterfall
- Funnel
- Ribbon
- Gauge
- Decomposition Tree

### 5. Interactive Features
Add slicers for Region, Category, Customer Segment, Channel and Date.

### 6. Detail
Create a Table, Matrix, Tooltip page and Drillthrough page.

### 7. Navigation
Create a Bookmark and a Button.

### 8. Final Dashboard
Arrange KPI cards at the top, followed by trend and comparison visuals, detailed analysis, and slicers.
