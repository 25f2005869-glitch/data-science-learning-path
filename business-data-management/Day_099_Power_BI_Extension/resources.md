## Study Topics
### Power Query
Data import, transformation, data types, cleaning and applied steps.

### DAX
Measures, SUM, DISTINCTCOUNT, DIVIDE, CALCULATE, filter context and time intelligence.

### Data Modelling
Fact tables, dimension tables, star schema, relationships, cardinality and filter direction.

### Visualisation
Cards, bar/column, line, area, pie/donut, scatter, treemap, waterfall, funnel, gauge, matrix and decomposition tree.

### Interactivity
Slicers, filters, tooltips, drillthrough, bookmarks, buttons and conditional formatting.
