## Management Cycle

**Plan → Organise → Lead → Monitor → Review → Improve**

## Key Management Areas

- Goal setting
- Target management
- Resource allocation
- Team coordination
- Budget management
- Performance monitoring
- KPI tracking
- Problem solving
- Review and improvement

## Suggested Workflow

1. Read the management dataset.
2. Understand the business functions.
3. Compare targets with actual results.
4. Review budgets and actual costs.
5. Calculate task completion.
6. Review customer satisfaction.
7. Compare business functions.
8. Identify areas requiring further investigation.
9. Create charts and dashboard views.
10. Write evidence-based observations.

## Important Data Practice

The workbook contains synthetic educational data. It is designed to practise business management and analytics concepts and should not be interpreted as real company performance.
