## Business Operations Flow

**Suppliers → Inputs → Business Processes → Outputs → Customers → Feedback**

A business uses people, money, materials, technology, information, and time to create products or services that provide value to customers.

## Major Business Functions

| Function | Main Role |
|---|---|
| Sales | Generates orders and revenue |
| Marketing | Creates awareness and demand |
| Operations | Produces or delivers the offering |
| Finance | Manages money, costs, and financial reporting |
| Human Resources | Manages employees and workforce needs |
| Procurement | Purchases goods and services |
| Customer Service | Handles customer support and feedback |
| Technology | Supports systems, data, and digital processes |

## Key Takeaways

- Businesses operate through connected processes.
- Every process consumes inputs and produces outputs.
- Different departments work together to create customer value.
- Revenue alone does not describe business performance.
- Cost, profit, volume, quality, time, and customer measures should be considered together.
- Business data helps managers understand what is happening in operations.
