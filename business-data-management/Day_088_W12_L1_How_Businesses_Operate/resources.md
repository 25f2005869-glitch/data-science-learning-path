## Suggested Workflow

1. Read the business process data.
2. Understand each column.
3. Review business functions and processes.
4. Calculate financial KPIs.
5. Calculate operational KPIs.
6. Compare processes.
7. Visualise the results.
8. Write evidence-based observations.

## Important Data Practice

Use the workbook as an educational practice dataset. The values are synthetic and are intended for learning spreadsheet and business analytics techniques, not for real business decisions.
