## MCQs

### 1. Which sequence best represents a basic business system?

A. Customers → Inputs → Suppliers → Process  
B. Inputs → Processes → Outputs  
C. Revenue → Profit → Inputs  
D. Cost → Customers → Suppliers

**Answer:** B

### 2. Which function mainly focuses on purchasing required goods and services?

A. Procurement  
B. Marketing  
C. Customer Service  
D. Sales

**Answer:** A

### 3. Which formula is correct?

A. Profit = Cost − Revenue  
B. Profit = Revenue + Cost  
C. Profit = Revenue − Cost  
D. Profit = Revenue × Cost

**Answer:** C

### 4. Which is an operational KPI?

A. Delivery Time  
B. Company Name  
C. Office Address  
D. Employee ID

**Answer:** A

### 5. Who normally receives the value created by a business?

A. Customer  
B. Spreadsheet  
C. Formula  
D. Dashboard

**Answer:** A
