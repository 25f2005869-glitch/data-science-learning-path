## 📌 What is Bottom-Up Estimation?

Bottom-Up estimation calculates a market by breaking it into smaller segments.

Instead of starting with one large market number, we estimate each segment and then add the results.

---

## 🧩 Market Segments

The workbook uses segments such as:

- Young Adults
- Working Families
- Rural Households
- Small Business Owners
- Other Households

Each segment has different assumptions.

---

## 🧮 Estimated Buyers

Formula:

**Estimated Buyers = Households × Potential Buyer Rate**

Example:

If a segment has 100,000 households and the potential buyer rate is 10%:

**Estimated Buyers = 100,000 × 10% = 10,000**

---

## 💰 Market Value

Formula:

**Market Value = Estimated Buyers × Average Bike Price**

This gives an estimate of the potential value of the two-wheeler market for a segment.

---

## 🏦 Loan Market

First estimate potential loan customers:

**Loan Customers = Estimated Buyers × Loan Rate**

Then:

**Potential Loan Market = Loan Customers × Average Loan Amount**

---

## 📊 Total Market

After calculating every segment:

**Total Market = Sum of all Segment Markets**

The same approach can be used for the loan market.

---

## 💡 Main Learning

Bottom-Up estimation follows:

**Segment → Assumption → Calculation → Segment Estimate → Total Market**

The quality of the final estimate depends heavily on the assumptions used.
