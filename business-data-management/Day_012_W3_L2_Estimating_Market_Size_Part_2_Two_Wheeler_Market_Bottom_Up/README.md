## 🎯 Learning Objective

Day 012 focuses on the **Bottom-Up approach** to estimating the potential two-wheeler market.

Instead of estimating the entire market directly, we divide the market into meaningful customer segments and estimate each segment separately.

## 🧮 Bottom-Up Approach

The basic process is:

**Customer Segments**

↓

**Number of Households**

↓

**Potential Buyer Rate**

↓

**Estimated Buyers**

↓

**Average Bike Price**

↓

**Estimated Market Value**

---

## 💰 Loan Market Estimation

Potential loan customers can be estimated using:

**Estimated Buyers × Loan Rate**

Potential loan market:

**Loan Customers × Average Loan Amount**
