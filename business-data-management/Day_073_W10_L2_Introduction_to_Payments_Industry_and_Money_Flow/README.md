# 📊 Day 073 — Introduction to Payments Industry and Money Flow

## 📌 Topic
Introduction to the payments industry and the movement of money through payment systems.

## 🎯 Objectives
1. Understand the payments ecosystem.
2. Identify common payment channels.
3. Distinguish transaction volume from transaction value.
4. Understand payment services.
5. Analyse transaction outcomes.
6. Calculate success and failure rates.
7. Compare payment channels.
8. Compare regions.
9. Compare payment services.
10. Build a payment-analysis dashboard.

## 💳 Payment Industry
Payments allow money to move between participants.
Payment activity generates transaction-level data.
Payment channels provide different ways to initiate transactions.
Payment services describe the business use case.

## 🔄 Money Flow
Payment initiation begins the transaction.
Authentication checks the relevant credentials or mechanism.
Authorization determines whether a transaction can proceed.
Processing moves the payment instruction through the system.
Settlement transfers funds between relevant participants.
Reconciliation compares records with expected transactions.

## 📊 Dataset
The workbook contains 40 illustrative payment records.
The dataset is synthetic and created for learning.
Payment channels include UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking and Wallet.
Regions include North, South, West and East.
Payment services include P2P, P2M, Bill Payment, E-commerce and Subscription.

## 📏 Measures
Transaction Volume.
Transaction Value.
Average Ticket Value.
Successful Transactions.
Failed Transactions.
Success Rate.
Failure Rate.

## 🧮 Formula
Success Rate = Successful Records / Total Records × 100.

Failure Rate = Failed Records / Total Records × 100.

Average Ticket Value = Transaction Value / Transaction Volume.
