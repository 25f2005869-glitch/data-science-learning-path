# 📝 Day 073 Notes — Payments Industry and Money Flow

## 1. Payment
A payment is a transfer of monetary value between relevant participants.

## 2. Payments Industry
The payments industry includes systems and services that facilitate money movement.

## 3. Payment Channel
A payment channel is the mechanism through which a transaction is initiated or processed.

## 4. UPI
UPI is an account-based digital payment mechanism used for electronic transactions.

## 5. Debit Card
A debit card transaction generally draws funds from a linked account.

## 6. Credit Card
A credit card transaction uses an approved credit facility.

## 7. Net Banking
Net banking enables customers to initiate payments through online banking interfaces.

## 8. Wallet
A wallet can provide a digital mechanism for storing or accessing payment value.

## 9. Payment Service
A payment service describes the use case represented by the transaction.

## 10. P2P
P2P represents person-to-person payment activity.

## 11. P2M
P2M represents person-to-merchant payment activity.

## 12. Bill Payment
Bill payment represents payment for a billed service or obligation.

## 13. E-commerce
E-commerce payments support purchases through online commerce channels.

## 14. Subscription
Subscription payments can represent recurring service payments.

## 15. Transaction Volume
Transaction volume measures the quantity of transactions.

## 16. Transaction Value
Transaction value measures the monetary amount represented by transactions.

## 17. Average Ticket Value
Average ticket value represents the average monetary amount per transaction.

## 18. Transaction Status
Transaction status records an outcome such as successful or failed.

## 19. Success Rate
Success rate measures the percentage of records marked successful.

## 20. Failure Rate
Failure rate measures the percentage of records marked failed.

## 21. Payment Initiation
The payment process begins when a participant initiates a transaction.

## 22. Authentication
Authentication checks whether the transaction request is appropriately authenticated.

## 23. Authorization
Authorization determines whether a requested transaction can proceed.

## 24. Processing
Processing handles the movement of transaction instructions.

## 25. Settlement
Settlement completes the relevant financial transfer between participants.

## 26. Reconciliation
Reconciliation compares transaction records with expected financial records.

## 27. Money Flow
Money flow describes movement of monetary value through the payment process.

## 28. Channel Mix
Channel mix shows the distribution of activity across payment channels.

## 29. Regional Analysis
Regional analysis compares payment activity across geographic groups.

## 30. Service Analysis
Service analysis compares transaction activity across payment use cases.

## 31. Volume vs Value
A high transaction volume does not necessarily mean high transaction value.

## 32. Ticket Value
Ticket value helps distinguish transaction frequency from transaction size.

## 33. Dashboard
A payment dashboard combines selected metrics for quick review.

## 34. Doughnut Chart
A doughnut chart can display channel composition.

## 35. Column Chart
A column chart can compare transaction value across channels.

## 36. Line Chart
A line chart can display values across an ordered set of regions or periods.

## 37. Scatter Plot
A scatter plot can examine the relationship between transaction volume and value.

## 38. Data Quality
Payment records should be checked for missing values and invalid categories.

## 39. Financial Data Governance
Real payment data requires security, access control and appropriate privacy practices.

## 40. Final Learning
Payment analysis connects transaction activity, monetary flow, channels, services and outcomes.
