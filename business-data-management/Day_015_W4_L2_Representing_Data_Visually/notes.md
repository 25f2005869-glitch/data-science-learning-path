**Topic:** W4_L2 — Representing Data Visually

## 1. 📊 Why Visualization?

Visualization converts raw numbers into an easier-to-understand visual form.

It helps us:

- Compare categories
- Identify patterns
- Understand trends
- Communicate business insights

## 2. 📊 Bar / Column Chart

Best for comparing categories.

**Example:** Region-wise sales.

## 3. 📈 Line Chart

Best when the order of observations matters, especially for time-based data.

**Example:** Monthly sales trend.

## 4. 🥧 Pie Chart

Best for showing parts of a meaningful whole.

**Example:** Product-wise sales share.

## 5. 🐍 Python Visualization

Common libraries:

- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## 6. 📗 Excel Visualization

Basic flow:

**Select Data → Insert → Choose Chart → Add Title → Format → Interpret**

## 💡 Key Idea

The goal is not just to create a beautiful chart.

The goal is to select a chart that answers the **business question clearly**.
