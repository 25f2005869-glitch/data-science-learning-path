## Data Quality Checklist

Check job IDs.

Check job titles.

Check department labels.

Check level definitions.

Check skill names.

Check experience units.

Check KPI descriptions.

Check reporting fields.

## Job Description Checklist

Is the role purpose clear?

Are responsibilities understandable?

Are skills role-relevant?

Is experience realistic?

Are qualifications relevant?

Are KPIs measurable?

Is the reporting relationship clear?

## Candidate Matching Extension

Map candidate skills to required skills.

Compare experience with the role requirement.

Check relevant performance evidence.

Review role-specific assessment results.

Document the matching criteria.

## Responsible Use

Do not treat a generic job description as a complete hiring model.

Do not use irrelevant criteria.

Keep requirements connected to the actual role.

Review descriptions when responsibilities change.
