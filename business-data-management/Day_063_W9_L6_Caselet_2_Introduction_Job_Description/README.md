# 📊 Day 063 — Caselet 2: Introduction — Job Description

## 📌 Day Overview

Day 063 introduces the structure and purpose of a job description in an internal sourcing caselet.

A job description converts a business requirement into a structured description of a role.

This package uses illustrative job descriptions for learning.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

1. Understand why organizations create job descriptions.
2. Identify the major components of a job description.
3. Distinguish responsibilities from skills.
4. Connect experience requirements to role level.
5. Connect responsibilities to measurable KPIs.
6. Understand reporting relationships.
7. Prepare a structured role profile for candidate matching.
8. Review job descriptions for completeness.

## 🧩 Main Components

- Job Title
- Department
- Function
- Level
- Qualification
- Required Skills
- Experience Required
- Role Summary
- Key KPIs
- Reports To

## 🔍 Why Job Descriptions Matter

A clear job description establishes what the role is expected to accomplish.

It gives candidates a consistent reference for understanding the opportunity.

It also provides structured inputs for internal candidate matching.

## 🏢 Organizational Context

The workbook contains examples across Analytics, Finance, HR, Sales, Operations, Marketing and other functions.

Business context is represented as B2B or B2C.

Job level is represented as Entry or Intermediate in this illustrative dataset.

## 🧪 Analysis Flow

1. Review the job description.
2. Identify role requirements.
3. Separate required and contextual information.
4. Count required skills.
5. Summarize experience requirements.
6. Compare departments and functions.
7. Review description completeness.
8. Prepare the role for candidate matching.

## ⚠️ Important Interpretation

The dataset is illustrative.

A job description should reflect the actual role rather than generic skill lists.

Requirements should be relevant, documented and periodically reviewed.

## 🧠 Key Takeaway

A good job description creates a structured bridge between a business need and a candidate profile.
