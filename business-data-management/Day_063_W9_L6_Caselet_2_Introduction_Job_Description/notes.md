# 📝 Day 063 Notes — Introduction to Job Description

## 1. Job Description

A job description is a structured representation of a role.

It communicates the purpose, requirements and expected outcomes of the role.

## 2. Job Title

The title provides a concise identity for the position.

It should reflect the nature and level of work.

## 3. Department

Department identifies where the role sits organizationally.

It helps connect the role with relevant stakeholders.

## 4. Function

Function describes the specific area of work.

Examples include Business Analytics, Financial Reporting and Customer Analytics.

## 5. Level

Level provides a broad indication of seniority.

The illustrative dataset uses Entry and Intermediate.

## 6. Qualification

Qualification describes the educational background expected for the role.

It should be aligned with actual job requirements.

## 7. Required Skills

Required skills describe capabilities needed for the role.

Skills may include technical, analytical or communication capabilities.

## 8. Experience

Experience indicates the expected prior exposure to similar work.

It should be connected to responsibility and complexity.

## 9. Role Summary

The role summary explains why the position exists.

It should communicate the main purpose rather than list every task.

## 10. Key KPIs

KPIs describe measurable outcomes associated with the role.

Connecting KPIs to responsibilities makes expectations clearer.

## 11. Reporting Relationship

Reports To identifies the manager or reporting structure.

This helps clarify organizational accountability.

## 12. Internal Sourcing

Internal candidates can be matched against structured job requirements.

The job description provides the target role profile.

## 13. Candidate Matching

Candidate matching can compare skills, experience and role requirements.

Matching should use relevant evidence rather than a single metric.

## 14. Completeness

A complete description contains the important role fields.

Completeness checks can identify missing information before sourcing begins.

## 15. Business Analysis

Department and function summaries help understand the role portfolio.

Skill frequency can show which capabilities recur across descriptions.

## 16. Excel Workflow

Filter the Job_Descriptions sheet.

Use summaries for department and level comparisons.

Use the dashboard for presentation.

## 17. Python Workflow

## 18. Practical Principle

A job description should describe the work that needs to be performed.

It should not be created only by copying a generic template.

## 19. Summary

Job descriptions organize role requirements.

They support communication, sourcing and candidate-role matching.

The quality of the role definition affects later analysis.
