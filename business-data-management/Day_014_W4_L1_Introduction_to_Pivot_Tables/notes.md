## 📌 What is a Pivot Table?

A Pivot Table is an Excel feature used to **summarize and analyse large amounts of data**.

It allows us to quickly calculate totals, counts and averages without manually writing many formulas.

---

## 🧩 Main Components

### Rows

Groups records according to a field.

Example:

`Category`

---

### Columns

Creates another dimension for comparison.

Example:

`Region`

---

### Values

Contains calculations such as:

- Sum
- Count
- Average

Example:

`Sum of Sales`

---

### Filters

Allows us to analyse only selected records.

Example:

`Electronics`

---

## 📊 Example

A sales dataset can be summarized by:

**Category → Total Sales**

or:

**Region → Total Sales**

or:

**Product → Quantity Sold**

## 🔄 Pivot Table Workflow

**Raw Data**

↓

**Insert Pivot Table**

↓

**Select Rows**

↓

**Select Values**

↓

**Apply Filters**

↓

**Analyse Summary**

---

## 💡 Key Learning

Pivot Tables make it easier to convert raw business data into a useful summary for analysis and decision-making.
