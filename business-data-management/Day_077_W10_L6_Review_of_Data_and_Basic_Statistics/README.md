# 📘 Day 077 — Review of Data and Basic Statistics

## 🎯 Objective
- Review the PayBuddy-style dataset before deeper analysis.
- Check structure, completeness and basic quality.
- Calculate descriptive statistics.
- Compare mean, median and spread.
- Connect statistics with business interpretation.

## 🧾 Dataset Context
- The dataset contains 30 synthetic player records.
- It represents a learning-oriented financial ecosystem.
- Player types include Bank, NBFC, Insurance, Capital Market and FinTech.
- Industries include Payments, Lending, Insurance, Investment and Compliance.
- Regions include North, South, West and East.

## 🔎 Data Review
- Inspect identifiers first.
- Check categorical fields.
- Check numeric fields.
- Check missing values.
- Check duplicate Player_ID values.
- Confirm measurement units.

## 📊 Main Measures
- Active customers are measured in lakh.
- Transactions are measured in lakh.
- Transaction value is measured in crore.
- Digital adoption is a percentage.
- Satisfaction is a synthetic score.

## 📐 Basic Statistics
- Count measures the number of observations.
- Mean describes the arithmetic average.
- Median identifies the middle observation after ordering.
- Standard deviation describes dispersion around the mean.
- Range equals maximum minus minimum.
- Q1 is the 25th percentile.
- Q3 is the 75th percentile.
- IQR equals Q3 minus Q1.

## 🧮 Derived Measures
- Transaction value per customer normalises transaction value.
- Transactions per customer normalises activity.
- Engagement Index combines digital adoption and satisfaction.
- Derived metrics should always retain their definitions.

## ⚠️ Interpretation Rule
- Mean and median answer different questions.
- A high transaction value does not imply high profit.
- Correlation does not prove causation.
- Synthetic data is not real market evidence.
- Always state the population and units.
