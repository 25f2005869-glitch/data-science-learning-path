## 7. Normalisation
- Raw totals depend on scale.
- Per-customer measures improve comparability.
- Ratios need consistent units.
- Always explain the denominator.

## 8. Statistical Interpretation
- Mean summarises average magnitude.
- Median identifies the central ordered observation.
- Standard deviation describes spread.
- IQR focuses on the middle half.
- Range shows total observed spread.

## 9. Relationship Analysis
- Scatter plots visualise two numeric variables.
- Correlation measures linear association.
- A relationship may be influenced by other variables.
- Statistical association is not proof of causality.

## 10. Dashboard Skills

## 12. Responsible Data Use
- Keep synthetic data labelled.
- Do not present illustrative values as real company statistics.
- Do not generalise from a small synthetic sample.
- Report the population represented by the dataset.
- Keep assumptions visible.
