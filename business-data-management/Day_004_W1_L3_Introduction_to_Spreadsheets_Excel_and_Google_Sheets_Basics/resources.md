## 🧠 4. Core Concepts for Revision

Before completing Day 004, make sure you understand:

### Spreadsheet

A tool for organizing and working with tabular data.

### Workbook

The spreadsheet file.

### Worksheet

An individual sheet inside a workbook.

### Row

Horizontal arrangement of cells.

### Column

Vertical arrangement of cells.

### Cell

Intersection of a row and a column.

### Cell Reference

The address used to identify a cell.

Example:

`A1`
