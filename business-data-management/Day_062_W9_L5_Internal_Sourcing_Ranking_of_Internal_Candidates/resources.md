## Data Quality Checklist

Check unique candidate IDs.

Check missing evaluation values.

Check valid score ranges.

Check consistent role labels.

Check experience units.

Check assessment scale.

Check selection labels.

## Analysis Checklist

Define the business question.

Define criteria.

Document weights.

Calculate score.

Validate ranking.

Compare segments.

Visualize results.

Document limitations.

## Responsible Analytics

Do not present illustrative data as company evidence.

Do not hide score definitions.

Do not imply causation from ranking.

Do not treat a single metric as complete evidence.

Keep decision criteria transparent.

## Limitations

The dataset is illustrative.

The weights are learning assumptions.

Selection labels are illustrative.

The ranking should not be used as an actual HR decision model.
