# 📝 Day 062 Notes — Ranking of Internal Candidates

## 1. Purpose

Internal sourcing identifies candidates already available within an organization.

Ranking helps organize a candidate pool using consistent criteria.

## 2. Candidate Data

Each candidate has an identifier, department, region, target role and experience.

Skills and evaluation scores provide additional evidence for comparison.

## 3. Performance Score

Performance represents an illustrative evaluation dimension.

It contributes 30% to the composite score.

## 4. Skill Match

Skill match measures alignment with the target role.

It contributes 30% to the composite score.

## 5. Leadership

Leadership contributes 20%.

It can capture a separate capability dimension from technical fit.

## 6. Experience

Experience is normalized to an illustrative 0–8 year scale.

It contributes 10%.

## 7. Assessment

Assessment score is treated as a 0–30 score.

It contributes 10% after normalization.

## 8. Composite Score

Weighted scoring combines the evaluation dimensions.

Higher composite score produces a higher ranking position.

## 9. Ranking

The rank is created from the composite score in descending order.

Rank 1 is the highest score.

## 10. Department Comparison

Department summaries show candidate counts and average scores.

This supports workforce planning questions.

## 11. Sourcing Comparison

Sourcing summaries compare internal transfer and internal promotion records.

Average composite score is one descriptive measure.

## 12. Selection Status

Selected and Not Selected are illustrative labels.

They should not be interpreted as actual employment decisions.

## 13. Excel Workflow

Review Candidate_Data first.

Inspect Candidate_Ranking next.

Use summaries to compare groups.

Use the dashboard for presentation.

## 14. Python Workflow

Read the workbook with pandas.

Calculate the composite score.

Create the rank.

Group by department, source and region.

Export reusable CSV files.

Create charts with matplotlib.

## 15. Interpretation

Weighted scores are sensitive to assumptions.

Different roles may need different weights.

Data quality should be checked before ranking.

## 16. Responsible Use

Do not hide evaluation criteria.

Document score definitions.

Avoid treating a single score as complete evidence.

Use appropriate human review for real decisions.

## 17. Summary

Internal candidate ranking converts multiple indicators into an organized comparison.

The technique is useful for learning structured business analytics.

Always connect the ranking to the actual business question.
