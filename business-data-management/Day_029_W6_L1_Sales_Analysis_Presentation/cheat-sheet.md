## Quick Definitions

- KPI = Key Performance Indicator.
- Orders = number of transactions.
- Units = quantity sold.
- Revenue = sales value.
- Trend = direction over time.
- Ranking = ordered comparison.
- Insight = meaningful interpretation.
- Dashboard = compact visual summary.

## Quick Formulas

- Total Orders = SUM(Orders).
- Total Units = SUM(Units).
- Total Revenue = SUM(Revenue).
- Revenue Share = Product Revenue / Total Revenue.
- Growth % = (Current - Previous) / Previous × 100.
- Average Order Value = Revenue / Orders.
- Units per Order = Units / Orders.

## Chart Selection

- Time trend → Line chart.
- Product ranking → Column chart.
- Channel comparison → Bar chart.
- Region comparison → Bar/column chart.
- KPI → KPI cards.
- Use a chart only when it improves understanding.

## Excel Quick Steps

- Filter data.
- Create PivotTable.
- Put Month in Rows.
- Put Revenue in Values.
- Repeat for Product.
- Repeat for Channel.
- Repeat for Region.
- Add charts.
- Check labels.

## Interpretation Rules

- Higher revenue means higher sales value, not necessarily higher profit.
- Higher units means higher volume.
- A rising line indicates an upward trend.
- Ranking depends on the selected metric.
- Correlation does not prove causation.
- Always state the metric and period.
