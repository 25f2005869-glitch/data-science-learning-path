## 🔹 1. Demographic Data

Demographic data describes the characteristics of people or a population.

Examples:

- Age
- Gender
- City
- Education
- Employment
- Household Size
- Income

---

## 🔹 2. Age & Age Groups

Age can be grouped to make analysis easier.

Example:

- 18–25
- 26–35
- 36–45
- 46–55

Age groups help businesses understand different customer segments.

---

## 🔹 3. Gender

Gender is a demographic variable that can be used to understand the composition of a dataset.

Example:

**Male / Female**

---

## 🔹 4. Location

City or geographic location can help identify where respondents or customers are located.

Example:

**Ranchi, Delhi, Patna, Kolkata**

---

## 🔹 5. Education & Employment

Education and employment provide additional information about the population.

Examples:

**Education:** Undergraduate, Graduate, Postgraduate

**Employment:** Student, Employed

---

## 🔹 6. Household Size

Household size represents the number of people living in a household.

Example:

**Household Size = 4**

---

## 🔹 7. Income

Monthly income can be used to understand the economic characteristics of respondents.

It can help businesses compare income levels across demographic groups.

---

`COUNTIF`
