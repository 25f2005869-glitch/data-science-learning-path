## 💰 Regional Revenue

Regional revenue represents the total revenue associated with a region.

The Region_Revenue sheet provides the regional baseline.

Regional revenue is useful for understanding geographic contribution.

## 👤 Sales Agent Revenue

Sales-agent revenue represents the revenue associated with each sales agent.

The Sales_Agent_Performance sheet provides the agent-level summary.

This allows agent contribution to be compared descriptively.

## 🔗 Region-Agent Analysis

Region-agent analysis combines geographic and sales ownership dimensions.

It helps show how revenue is distributed across assignments.

The Region_Agent_Summary sheet provides this view.

## 🗺️ Agent Coverage

Regions_Covered represents the number of unique regions associated with an agent.

Coverage is a descriptive measure.

It should not automatically be interpreted as workload quality.

## 📅 Monthly Agent Revenue

Monthly_Agent_Revenue provides a time-based view.

It helps examine changes in agent revenue across the observation period.

## 📈 Revenue Share

Revenue share measures contribution to total revenue.

Formula:

Revenue Share = Revenue / Total Revenue

The result is generally expressed as a percentage.

## 💹 Financial Context

Revenue and gross margin provide different information.

Gross margin in this practice dataset is:

Gross Margin = Revenue - Production Cost

Margin percentage is:

Margin % = Gross Margin / Revenue

## 🧠 Planning Context

Planning should use evidence from revenue, coverage, trends and relevant business metrics.

The workbook provides analytical evidence rather than an automatic staffing decision.
