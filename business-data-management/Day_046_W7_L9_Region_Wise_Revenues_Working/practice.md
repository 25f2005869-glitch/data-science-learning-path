# 🧪 Day 046 Practice — Region-wise Revenues

## 🎯 Practice Goal

Use the prepared workbook to perform a complete regional revenue analysis.

Answer each task using evidence from the relevant worksheet.

## Level 1 — Understand

1. Open Revenue_Data.
2. Identify the Region field.
3. List the regions.
4. Identify the Revenue field.
5. Open Data_Dictionary.
6. Read the Revenue definition.
7. Identify the monthly periods.
8. Identify the Product field.
9. Identify Customer_Type.
10. Identify Gross_Margin.

## Level 2 — Regional Aggregation

11. Open Region_Revenue.
12. Compare regional revenue.
13. Check Revenue_Rank.
14. Verify regional totals.
15. Compare gross margin.
16. Compare margin percentage.
17. Compare sales conversion.
18. Compare scrap rate.
19. Compare closing inventory.
20. Record the major regional differences.

## Level 3 — Revenue Share

21. Open Revenue_Share.
22. Record the revenue share of each region.
23. Check that shares total approximately 100%.
24. Identify the largest revenue share.
25. Explain revenue concentration.
26. Compare revenue share with revenue ranking.
27. Write one evidence-based observation.
28. Record the supporting worksheet.
29. Record the supporting number.
30. Check your calculation.

## Level 4 — Monthly Analysis

38. Compare the regional trends.
39. Write one trend statement.
40. Support the statement with data.

## Level 5 — Product-region Analysis

48. Compare products within South.
49. Compare products within West.
