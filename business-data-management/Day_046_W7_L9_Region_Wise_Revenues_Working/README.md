## 💰 Revenue Analysis

Regional revenue is the total revenue generated by records belonging to a specific region.

The regional summary provides a compact management view.

## 📊 Revenue Share

Revenue Share shows how much each region contributes to the total revenue.

Formula:

Regional Revenue / Total Revenue

The result is normally expressed as a percentage.

## 🏆 Ranking

Revenue ranking orders regions according to total revenue.

Ranking provides a quick comparison but does not explain the reason behind a result.

## 📅 Monthly Analysis

Monthly regional revenue adds a time dimension to the analysis.

A line chart can be used to observe changes across months.

## 🏭 Product-region Analysis

Product-region analysis combines product and region.

It helps identify whether regional revenue is concentrated in particular products.

## 💹 Business Context

Revenue should not automatically be treated as profit.

Gross margin and margin percentage provide additional financial context.

Sales conversion, scrap rate, and inventory provide operational context.
