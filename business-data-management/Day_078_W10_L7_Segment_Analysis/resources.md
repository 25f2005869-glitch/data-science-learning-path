- Region × segment.

- These combinations reveal structure beyond a single grouping.

## 7. Normalisation

- Totals are influenced by segment size.
- Per-customer measures adjust for customer scale.
- Ratios require consistent units.
- A ratio is only meaningful when its denominator is appropriate.

## 8. Visualisation

- Column charts compare segment magnitudes.
- Line charts can compare ordered categories.
- Scatter charts show two numeric variables.
- Cross-tabs can be displayed using grouped charts.

## 9. Data Quality

- Verify unique IDs.
- Check missing segment labels.
- Check numeric completeness.
- Validate categories.
- Validate units.
- Reconcile aggregate totals.

## 10. Interpretation

- Describe observed patterns.
- Avoid unsupported causal claims.
- Distinguish synthetic data from real evidence.
- Use both totals and normalised metrics.
- State the limitations of the dataset.
