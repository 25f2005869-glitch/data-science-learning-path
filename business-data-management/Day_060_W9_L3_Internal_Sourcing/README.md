# 🏢 Day 060 — Internal Sourcing

## 📌 Day Overview

Day 060 — W9_L3 introduces **Internal Sourcing** as a business process.
This package uses an illustrative internal-request dataset for practical business-data analysis.
The dataset is educational and is not official IIT Madras or company data.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

1. Understand the idea of internal sourcing.
2. Identify internal teams that fulfill business requirements.
3. Analyze internal requests by department, source and region.
4. Compare planned and actual completion time.
5. Calculate cost and completion metrics.
6. Practice evidence-based operational interpretation.

## 🏢 Internal Sourcing

Internal sourcing refers to using capabilities available within an organization to fulfill a requirement.
An internal team may provide technology, analytics, HR, finance, security, operations or other services.
The exact organizational structure differs across companies.

## 📊 Dataset

The workbook contains 24 illustrative internal sourcing requests.
Fields include requesting department, requirement, internal source, region, month, internal cost, planned days, actual days, satisfaction and completion.
Calculated fields include time variance, on-time flag and cost per day.

## 📈 Key Metrics

Request count describes demand for internal services.
Total internal cost summarizes recorded cost.
Average request cost describes typical cost in the sample.
Time variance = Actual Days − Planned Days.
Completion Rate = Completed Requests ÷ Total Requests × 100.
On-Time Rate = Requests meeting the planned-day threshold ÷ Total Requests × 100.
Average satisfaction summarizes the recorded service-experience score.

## 🧪 Analysis Flow

Business question → data → quality check → aggregation → visualization → interpretation.
Start with the raw data before reading the dashboard.
Validate totals and definitions before communicating results.

## ⚠️ Interpretation

Internal sourcing metrics describe this illustrative dataset.
A higher cost does not automatically mean inefficient sourcing.
A time variance can identify a difference between plan and actual duration but does not establish its cause.
Satisfaction scores require a clear measurement method in real organizations.

## 🐍 Python

The Python script uses pandas for grouping and aggregation.
It calculates time variance and on-time status.
It exports CSV summaries.
It creates four PNG charts with matplotlib.
