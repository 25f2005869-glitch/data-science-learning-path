# 📝 Day 060 Notes — Internal Sourcing

## 1. Meaning of Internal Sourcing

Internal sourcing means using organizational capabilities to fulfill a requirement.
An internal source can be a specialized team or function.
Internal sourcing is different from obtaining a service from an external supplier.

## 2. Internal Source Teams

Internal IT teams can support technology requirements.
Internal analytics teams can support reporting and data requirements.
Internal HR teams can support people-related requirements.
Internal finance teams can support finance-related work.
Internal security teams can support security-related requirements.
Internal L&D teams can support learning requirements.

## 3. Requesting Department

The requesting department creates or owns a business requirement.
Department-level analysis can show where internal demand is concentrated.
Demand should be interpreted using the organization's operating context.

## 4. Cost

Internal cost represents the illustrative cost recorded for a request.
Total cost can be grouped by department or internal source.
Average cost can help compare typical request size.

## 5. Planned and Actual Time

Planned Days represent expected completion duration.
Actual Days represent observed completion duration.
Time Variance = Actual Days − Planned Days.
Positive variance means actual duration exceeded planned duration.
Zero variance means actual duration matched planned duration.
Negative variance means actual duration was below the plan.

## 6. Completion

Completed_Flag records whether a request is marked completed.
Completion Rate provides a simple summary of completed requests.
The definition and observation period should be consistent.

## 7. On-Time Work

On-Time Flag is 1 when actual days are at or below planned days.
On-Time Rate summarizes the proportion meeting that threshold.
An on-time metric does not by itself measure quality.

## 8. Satisfaction

Internal Satisfaction is an illustrative 1-to-5 score.
Average satisfaction can be compared by department or source.
Real satisfaction metrics require a defined survey methodology.

## 9. Excel Workflow

Inspect the raw data.
Filter departments and sources.
Create summary tables.
Calculate cost and time metrics.
Use charts to communicate patterns.
Validate calculations.

## 10. Python Workflow

Read the Excel workbook with pandas.
Calculate time variance.
Create department summaries.
Create source summaries.
Create regional and monthly summaries.
Export CSV files.
Create matplotlib charts.

## 11. Interpretation

Descriptive analysis tells us what is recorded in the data.
It does not automatically explain why a result occurred.
Operational context should be considered before making conclusions.

## 12. Day 060 Takeaways

Internal sourcing uses internal organizational capability.
Requests can be analyzed by department, source, region and month.
Cost, time, completion and satisfaction are useful measures.
Planned versus actual analysis can reveal timing differences.
Careful definitions improve the quality of business analysis.
