## 6. Excel Remove Duplicates

Use this feature to identify and remove genuine duplicate records.

Practice:

1. Select the dataset.
2. Open the Data tab.
3. Select Remove Duplicates.
4. Choose the columns to check.
5. Review the result.

Official resource:

https://support.microsoft.com/excel

## 📌 Key Takeaway

**Good business analysis starts with good data quality.**

The purpose of data cleaning is not simply to make a dataset look neat. It is to make the data consistent, reliable and suitable for meaningful business analysis.
