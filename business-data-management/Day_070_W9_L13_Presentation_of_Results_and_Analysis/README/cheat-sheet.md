## Presentation Rules

Start with the business question.

Keep the dataset explanation short.

Show important KPIs early.

Use relevant comparisons.

Use readable charts.

Label units.

State the period.

Separate evidence from interpretation.

Mention limitations.

## Evidence

Use measurable values.

Identify the dimension.

Identify the time period.

Avoid unsupported claims.

## Observation

State what changed.

State what differed.

State the relevant metric.

## Interpretation

Explain the analytical meaning.

Keep the interpretation connected to evidence.

Do not claim causation without supporting evidence.

## Action

Suggest a possible next investigation.

Connect action to the observed issue.

Keep action measurable where possible.

## Common Mistakes

Too many charts.

Unclear titles.

Missing units.

No time period.

Unsupported conclusions.

Confusing correlation with causation.

Hiding limitations.
