## Insight Writing Template

Observation:
State the pattern.

Evidence:
Mention the relevant metric.

Context:
Specify the population and period.

Interpretation:
Explain the analytical meaning.

Action:
State what could be investigated next.

## Communication Rules

Avoid unnecessary detail.

Avoid unsupported causal claims.

Avoid confusing correlation with causation.

Avoid unlabeled metrics.

Avoid ambiguous chart titles.

Avoid hiding limitations.
