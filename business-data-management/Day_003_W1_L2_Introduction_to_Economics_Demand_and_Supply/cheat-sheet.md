## 🧠 1. Demand

**Demand = Willingness + Ability to Buy**

Demand represents the consumer side of the market.

### Key Points

- Consumer wants the product.
- Consumer has the ability to pay.
- Consumer is willing to buy at a particular price.

---

## 📊 2. Quantity Demanded

**Quantity Demanded = Specific quantity consumers buy at a particular price**

Example:

**Price = ₹100 → Quantity Demanded = 100 units**

---

## 📉 3. Law of Demand

When other factors remain constant:

**Price ↑ → Quantity Demanded ↓**

**Price ↓ → Quantity Demanded ↑**

### Relationship

**Inverse Relationship**

---

## 📈 4. Demand Curve

- Y-axis → **Price**
- X-axis → **Quantity Demanded**
- Normally slopes **downward**

**Demand Curve ↓**

---

## 🔍 5. Factors Affecting Demand

| Factor | Possible Effect |
|---|---|
| Price | Affects quantity demanded |
| Income | Can increase/decrease demand |
| Preferences | Changes consumer demand |
| Price of substitutes | Can change demand |
| Price of complements | Can change demand |
| Expectations | Can change current demand |
| Number of consumers | Can change market demand |

---

## 🔄 6. Demand vs Quantity Demanded

### Demand

Overall relationship between price and quantity.

### Quantity Demanded

Specific quantity at a particular price.

### Remember

**Own Price Change → Movement Along Demand Curve**

**Other Factors Change → Demand Curve Shift**

---

# 🏭 7. Supply

**Supply = Willingness + Ability of Producers to Sell**

Supply represents the producer side of the market.

---

## 📊 8. Quantity Supplied

**Quantity Supplied = Specific quantity producers sell at a particular price**

Example:

**Price = ₹100 → Quantity Supplied = 20 units**

---

## 📈 9. Law of Supply

When other factors remain constant:

**Price ↑ → Quantity Supplied ↑**

**Price ↓ → Quantity Supplied ↓**

### Relationship

**Direct / Positive Relationship**

---

## 📈 10. Supply Curve

- Y-axis → **Price**
- X-axis → **Quantity Supplied**
- Normally slopes **upward**

**Supply Curve ↑**

---

## 🔍 11. Factors Affecting Supply

| Factor | Possible Effect |
|---|---|
| Price | Affects quantity supplied |
| Production Cost | Can affect supply |
| Technology | Can increase efficiency/supply |
| Resource Availability | Can affect supply |
| Expectations | Can affect current supply |
| Number of Sellers | Can change market supply |

---

## 🔄 12. Supply vs Quantity Supplied

### Supply

Overall relationship between price and quantity supplied.

### Quantity Supplied

Specific quantity supplied at a particular price.

### Remember

**Own Price Change → Movement Along Supply Curve**

**Other Factors Change → Supply Curve Shift**

---

# ⚖️ 13. Market Equilibrium

Equilibrium occurs when:

**Quantity Demanded = Quantity Supplied**

### Formula

**QD = QS**

### Equilibrium has:

- **Equilibrium Price**
- **Equilibrium Quantity**

---

# 📉 14. Shortage

Shortage occurs when:

**Quantity Demanded > Quantity Supplied**

### Formula

**Shortage = QD − QS**

Example:

**QD = 100**

**QS = 60**

**Shortage = 40 units**

---

# 📈 15. Surplus

Surplus occurs when:

**Quantity Supplied > Quantity Demanded**

### Formula

**Surplus = QS − QD**

Example:

**QS = 100**

**QD = 60**

**Surplus = 40 units**

---

# 🔄 16. Demand Curve Shifts

### Demand Increase

**Demand ↑ → Curve shifts Right →**

Possible causes:

- Income ↑
- Number of consumers ↑
- Favorable preferences
- Price of substitute ↑
- Price of complement ↓

### Demand Decrease

**Demand ↓ → Curve shifts Left ←**

Possible causes:

- Income ↓
- Number of consumers ↓
- Unfavorable preferences
- Price of substitute ↓
- Price of complement ↑

---

# 🔄 17. Supply Curve Shifts

### Supply Increase

**Supply ↑ → Curve shifts Right →**

Possible causes:

- Technology improves
- Production cost decreases
- Resources become more available
- Number of sellers increases

### Supply Decrease

**Supply ↓ → Curve shifts Left ←**

Possible causes:

- Production cost increases
- Resources become less available
- Production disruption
- Number of sellers decreases

---

# 🧠 18. Most Important Relationships

| Concept | Relationship |
|---|---|
| Price ↑ → Demand | Quantity Demanded ↓ |
| Price ↓ → Demand | Quantity Demanded ↑ |
| Price ↑ → Supply | Quantity Supplied ↑ |
| Price ↓ → Supply | Quantity Supplied ↓ |
| Equilibrium | QD = QS |
| Shortage | QD > QS |
| Surplus | QS > QD |

---

# 🎯 19. One-Line Revision

**Demand → Buyer Side**

**Supply → Seller Side**

**Law of Demand → Price ↑, QD ↓**

**Law of Supply → Price ↑, QS ↑**

**Equilibrium → QD = QS**

**Shortage → QD > QS**

**Surplus → QS > QD**

**Own Price Change → Movement Along Curve**

**Other Factor Change → Curve Shift**
