## 📚 Topics Covered

### 1️⃣ Demand

Understanding the concept of demand and how consumers respond to changes in market conditions.

### 2️⃣ Quantity Demanded

Understanding the amount of a product or service consumers are willing and able to purchase at a particular price.

### 3️⃣ Law of Demand

Understanding the relationship between **price and quantity demanded**.

### 4️⃣ Demand Curve

Understanding the graphical representation of the relationship between price and quantity demanded.

### 5️⃣ Factors Affecting Demand

Important factors that can influence demand include:

- Price
- Income
- Consumer preferences
- Prices of related goods
- Consumer expectations
- Number of consumers

### 6️⃣ Supply

Understanding how producers respond to market conditions and determine the quantity of goods or services they are willing to provide.

### 7️⃣ Quantity Supplied

Understanding the quantity producers are willing and able to sell at a particular price.

### 8️⃣ Law of Supply

Understanding the relationship between **price and quantity supplied**.

### 9️⃣ Supply Curve

Understanding the graphical representation of the relationship between price and quantity supplied.

### 🔟 Factors Affecting Supply

Understanding the factors that influence the supply of goods and services.

### 1️⃣1️⃣ Market Equilibrium

Understanding the point where **Demand and Supply meet** in a market.

### 1️⃣2️⃣ Shortage and Surplus

Understanding what happens when:

- Demand > Supply → **Shortage / Excess Demand**
- Supply > Demand → **Surplus / Excess Supply**

## 🧠 Key Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Demand** | Consumer willingness and ability to purchase |
| **Quantity Demanded** | Quantity consumers demand at a particular price |
| **Law of Demand** | Price and quantity demanded generally move in opposite directions |
| **Supply** | Producer willingness and ability to sell |
| **Quantity Supplied** | Quantity producers supply at a particular price |
| **Law of Supply** | Price and quantity supplied generally move in the same direction |
| **Equilibrium** | Point where demand and supply are balanced |
| **Shortage** | Demand is greater than supply |
| **Surplus** | Supply is greater than demand |
