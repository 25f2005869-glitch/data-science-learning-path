## 🎯 Core Idea

**Business Data Management** connects **business activities, data, analysis, insights, and decision-making**.

---

## 🔄 Data-to-Decision Flow

**Business Activity → Data → Analysis → Insight → Decision**

---

## 🧠 Key Concepts

### Business Activity
An activity performed by a business, such as sales, recruitment, manufacturing, finance, or inventory management.

### Business Data
Data generated or used during business activities.

### Data Analysis
The process of examining and analysing data to obtain useful information.

### Business Insight
Useful understanding obtained from analysing business data.

### Decision-Making
Using business information and insights to support decisions.

## 📈 Business Analysis Process

**1. Business Problem**

Understand what the business needs to solve.

**2. Data**

Identify the data relevant to the problem.

**3. Organisation**

Prepare and organise the data.

**4. Analysis**

Analyse the available data.

**5. Insight**

Identify useful findings.

**6. Decision**

Use the findings to support business decisions.
