## 🎯 Topic

Day 023 moves into detailed e-commerce sales data analysis.

The case uses business measures and dimensions to understand performance.

Post-COVID context provides the setting for the analysis.

The dataset should be interpreted within the case-study context.

## 🛒 E-Commerce Data

E-commerce data can describe products, customers, channels, regions, orders, units, discounts, and revenue.

Different dimensions allow business performance to be viewed from multiple perspectives.

Monthly analysis helps study changes over time.

Category analysis helps compare product groups.

Channel analysis helps compare sales platforms.

Regional analysis helps compare geographic markets.

Customer-type analysis helps compare customer groups.

## 💰 Revenue

Revenue is a central measure in the case.

Total revenue provides an overall view.

Monthly revenue shows time-based performance.

Category revenue shows product contribution.

Channel revenue shows platform contribution.

Regional revenue shows geographic contribution.

Customer revenue shows group contribution.

Revenue should not be interpreted as profit.

## 📦 Orders and Units

Orders measure transaction activity.

Units measure sales volume.

The two measures are related but not identical.

Comparing them can provide additional business context.

## 🏷️ Discount

Discount percentage provides pricing context.

Discount can be compared across categories and channels.

A high discount does not automatically mean better performance.

Revenue, orders, and units should be considered together.

## 🧠 Post-COVID Interpretation

The case provides a post-COVID business context.

Monthly trends can be examined in that context.

The practice dataset alone does not prove that COVID caused an observed change.

Use descriptive language unless stronger evidence is available.
