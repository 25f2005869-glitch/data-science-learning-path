## ⚡ Definitions

Revenue → Sales value in the dataset.

Orders → Transaction activity.

Units → Product volume.

Category → Product grouping.

Channel → Sales platform.

Region → Geographic grouping.

Customer Type → Customer classification.

Discount → Percentage reduction.

## 📊 Quick Analysis

Monthly Revenue → Group Revenue_Lakh by Month.

Category Revenue → Group Revenue_Lakh by Category.

Channel Revenue → Group Revenue_Lakh by Channel.

Regional Revenue → Group Revenue_Lakh by Region.

Customer Revenue → Group Revenue_Lakh by Customer_Type.

Monthly Orders → Group Orders by Month.

Category Units → Group Units by Category.

Average Discount → Mean of Discount_Percent.

## 🧮 Excel

SUM → Total.

AVERAGE → Average.

SUMIF → Conditional sum.

SUMIFS → Multiple-condition sum.

Pivot Table → Summary.

Column Chart → Comparison.

Line Chart → Trend.

Pie Chart → Simple composition.

## 🐍 Python

`pd.read_excel()` → Load Excel.

`df.head()` → Preview.

`df.shape` → Dimensions.

`df.isna().sum()` → Missing values.

`df.groupby()` → Group analysis.

`to_csv()` → Export summary.

`plt.bar()` → Bar chart.

`plt.savefig()` → Save chart.
