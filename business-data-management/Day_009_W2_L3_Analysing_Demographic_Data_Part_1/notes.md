## 🔹 1. Demographic Analysis

Demographic data can be combined with spending information to understand how different groups consume products and services.

Examples:

- Age Group
- Gender
- Location
- Household Size
- Income

---

## 🔹 2. Consumption Data

The practical dataset contains spending on:

- Food
- Clothing
- Transport

These can be combined to calculate **Total Consumption**.

**Total Consumption = Food + Clothing + Transport**

---

## 🔹 3. Group Comparison

Consumption can be compared across:

- Age groups
- Urban and Rural locations
- Income levels
- Household characteristics

---

## 🔹 4. Excel Analysis

Useful functions:

- `COUNTIF`
- `AVERAGE`
- `AVERAGEIF`
- `MIN`
- `MAX`

Example:

`=AVERAGEIF(B2:B16,"26-35",J2:J16)`

This calculates average consumption for the selected age group.
