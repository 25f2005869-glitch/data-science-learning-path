## 💼 BDM Connection

Demographic information can be combined with consumption data to understand how different groups behave.

**Demographic Data → Consumption Data → Comparison → Pattern → Business Insight**

## 🧠 Key Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Consumption | Spending/use of goods or services |
| Consumption Pattern | How spending differs across people or groups |
| Demographic Group | Group based on characteristics such as age or location |
| Total Consumption | Combined spending across selected categories |
