## 📌 Introduction

Trend analysis studies movement across an ordered sequence.

Time is commonly used as the ordering variable.

Sales data can be analyzed daily.

It can also be analyzed weekly or monthly.

Day 028 focuses on sales and revenue trends.

The analysis continues the e-commerce case study.

## 🎯 Purpose of Trend Analysis

Trend analysis helps identify direction.

The direction may be increasing.

The direction may be decreasing.

The direction may also remain relatively stable.

The objective is not only to calculate totals.

The objective is to understand movement.

Movement can help management identify areas requiring attention.

## 📅 Date Analysis

Date is an important business dimension.

Dates allow transactions to be placed chronologically.

Chronological ordering is important for trend analysis.

Incorrect ordering can produce misleading charts.

Date fields should therefore be checked before analysis.

The workbook contains a Date field.

The Month field supports monthly grouping.

The Weekday field supports weekday analysis.

## 📈 Monthly Analysis

Monthly analysis aggregates sales observations by month.

Orders can be summed.

Units can be summed.

Revenue can be summed.

The resulting table provides a compact trend view.

Monthly values should be arranged chronologically.

A line chart can then show the movement clearly.

## 💰 Revenue Trend

Revenue represents sales value.

Revenue trends show how sales value changes.

A rising revenue trend indicates increasing revenue.

A falling revenue trend indicates declining revenue.

A changing trend may require further investigation.

The chart alone does not explain the reason.

Additional product and channel analysis is useful.

## 📦 Units Trend

Units represent quantities sold.

Unit volume provides a demand perspective.

Units can increase while revenue increases.

Units can also increase while revenue grows more slowly.

Product prices and product mix can affect this relationship.

Therefore units should not be treated as revenue.

## 📊 Orders Trend

Orders indicate transaction volume.

Orders can be compared with units.

One order may contain multiple units.

Therefore Orders and Units can have different values.

This difference can provide useful business information.

## 📅 Weekday Analysis

Weekday analysis compares sales by day name.

The analysis can identify stronger and weaker weekdays.

A bar chart is useful for categorical weekday comparisons.

Weekday analysis is not the same as monthly trend analysis.

Month has chronological order.

Weekday is a categorical grouping.

## 🧮 Growth Rate

Growth compares current performance with previous performance.

Growth percentage can be calculated as:

(Current − Previous) / Previous × 100

A positive result indicates an increase.

A negative result indicates a decrease.

The first period normally has no previous comparison.

## 📊 Month-over-Month Growth

Month-over-month growth compares consecutive months.

January is compared with the next available month.

February is compared with March.

March is compared with April.

April is compared with May.

May is compared with June.

The calculation helps identify acceleration or decline.

## 📉 Reading a Trend

A rising line indicates upward movement.

A falling line indicates downward movement.

Repeated rises can indicate a sustained increase.

Repeated falls can indicate a sustained decline.

Alternating rises and falls indicate volatility.

Trend interpretation should consider the complete sequence.

## 🔍 Revenue Versus Units

Revenue and units provide different information.

Units measure quantity.

Revenue measures value.

A product can sell many units but generate modest revenue.

A premium product can sell fewer units but generate high revenue.

This is why both metrics should be considered.

## 🛍️ Product Context

Product-level analysis explains contribution.

Some products may contribute more revenue.

Other products may contribute more units.

Product trends can help explain monthly movement.

The Product_Trend sheet provides this comparison.

## 🌐 Channel Context

Channel analysis compares Website, App and Marketplace.

Different channels can contribute different revenue levels.

Channel trends can help identify concentration.

Management can use this information for channel planning.

## 🗺️ Regional Context

Region analysis compares geographic areas.

The workbook contains North, South, East and West.

Regional revenue can differ.

Regional unit volume can also differ.

The regional view adds context to overall sales movement.

## 📊 Excel Visualization

Line charts are suitable for time trends.

Bar charts are suitable for categorical comparisons.

Chart titles should be clear.

Axes should have meaningful labels.

Units should be stated where required.

Colours should make charts easy to read.

## 🧠 Business Interpretation

A trend is evidence of movement.

It is not automatically evidence of causation.

A high-sales month can have many possible explanations.

Promotions may influence sales.

Seasonality may influence sales.

Product mix may influence revenue.

These explanations should be treated as hypotheses unless supported by data.

## 🧹 Data Quality

Check missing values.

Check duplicate rows.

Check date formats.

Check numeric columns.

Check month ordering.

Check unusual values.

Data quality should be reviewed before interpreting trends.
