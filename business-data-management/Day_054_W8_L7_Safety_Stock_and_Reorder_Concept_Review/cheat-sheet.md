## ⚡ Key Formulas

`Lead-Time Demand = Average Daily Demand × Average Lead Time`

`Reorder Point = Lead-Time Demand + Safety Stock`

`Reorder Coverage Days = Reorder Point ÷ Average Daily Demand`

## 📌 Key Definitions

**Safety Stock:** Buffer inventory.

**Reorder Point:** Replenishment trigger level.

**Lead-Time Demand:** Expected demand while waiting for replenishment.

**Lead Time:** Replenishment waiting period.

**Demand Variability:** Variation in demand.

**Stockout Risk:** Risk of insufficient inventory before replenishment.
