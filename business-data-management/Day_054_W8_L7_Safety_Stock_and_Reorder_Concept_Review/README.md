## 🎯 Objective

Day 054 reviews two important inventory-management concepts: safety stock and reorder point.

The workbook uses an illustrative manufacturing inventory dataset.

The data is designed for learning and is not official IIT Madras data or actual company performance.

The practical goal is to connect demand, lead time, safety stock and replenishment decisions.

## 📌 Core Concepts

Safety stock is a buffer quantity.

Reorder point is the inventory level used as a replenishment trigger.

Lead-time demand represents expected demand during the replenishment waiting period.

## 🧮 Core Formula

`Lead-Time Demand = Average Daily Demand × Average Lead Time`

`Reorder Point = Lead-Time Demand + Safety Stock`
