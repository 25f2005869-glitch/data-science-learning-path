## 📌 Overview

HR Analytics is the process of analysing employee and workforce data to understand workforce structure, salary patterns, experience, departments, job roles, and employee attrition.

In this project, HR data is inspected, cleaned, analysed, summarised, and visualised to produce useful business insights.

The project uses **Excel** for data cleaning, calculations, analysis, KPI preparation, pivot-style summaries, and charts.

## 🧮 Important HR Metrics

### Average Salary

`Average Salary = Total Salary / Number of Employees`

### Attrition Rate

`Attrition Rate = Attrition Count / Total Employees × 100`

### Average Experience

`Average Experience = Total Experience / Number of Employees`

### Total Monthly Salary Cost

`Total Monthly Salary Cost = Sum of Monthly Salaries`
