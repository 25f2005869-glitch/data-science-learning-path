## 🌐 What Is E-Commerce?

E-commerce refers to business activity conducted through digital or online channels.

Customers can discover products digitally.

Customers can compare available products.

Customers can place orders through digital platforms.

Businesses can receive and process those orders.

Payments can also be handled through digital systems.

The business therefore generates data throughout the customer journey.

This data can be analysed for business decision-making.

---

## 🏪 E-Commerce Business View

An e-commerce business can be viewed through several dimensions.

One dimension is **time**.

Month is used as the time dimension in this workbook.

Another dimension is **product category**.

Category allows different product groups to be compared.

Another dimension is **sales channel**.

Channel identifies how the customer reaches the business.

Region provides a geographical dimension.

Combining these dimensions makes business analysis more useful.

---

## 📊 Business Dimensions

The workbook contains several dimensions.

### Month

Month allows performance to be studied over time.

### Category

Category groups products into business segments.

### Channel

Channel identifies the digital route used for sales.

### Region

Region provides a geographical comparison.

These dimensions can be used individually or together.

## 📈 Business Measures

The main measures in the workbook are:

- Orders
- Units
- Revenue

Orders represent the number of orders.

Units represent the number of units sold.

Revenue represents the monetary value generated in the sample.

These measures should be analysed together.

One measure alone may not provide enough business context.

---

## 📦 Orders

Orders are an important e-commerce performance measure.

A higher number of orders generally indicates greater transaction activity.

Orders can be grouped by month.

Orders can also be grouped by category.

They can be compared across channels.

They can also be compared across regions.

The workbook uses order counts for practical analysis.

---

## 🔢 Units

Units represent the quantity of products sold.

Units provide a different perspective from order count.

One order may contain multiple units.

Therefore, orders and units do not necessarily move identically.

Analysing both can provide better business understanding.

Units can be aggregated by month, category, channel and region.

---

## 💰 Revenue

Revenue is another important e-commerce measure.

The workbook records revenue in lakh units.

Revenue can be compared between categories.

It can also be compared between digital channels.

Monthly revenue can show the direction of business performance.

Revenue should be interpreted together with orders and units.

A website can act as a direct digital sales channel.

## 🧮 Average Revenue per Order

Average revenue per order is calculated as:

**Total Revenue ÷ Total Orders**

This provides a simple measure of revenue associated with each order on average.

It should not automatically be interpreted as individual customer spending.

It is a summary metric based on the sample dataset.

It can be useful when comparing business periods.

## 🧠 Business Interpretation

Data analysis is not only about calculating numbers.

The analyst should understand what the numbers represent.

A higher revenue value indicates greater monetary contribution in the sample.

A higher order count indicates greater transaction volume.

A higher unit count indicates greater quantity sold.

These measures can tell different stories.

Therefore, they should be interpreted together.
