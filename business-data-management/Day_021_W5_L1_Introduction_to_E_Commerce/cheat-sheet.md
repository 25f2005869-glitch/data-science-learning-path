## 🔑 Core Idea
E-commerce = business activity through digital or online channels.
Digital transactions generate business data.
Business data can be grouped, aggregated and visualised.

## 🧮 Important Formula
**Average Revenue per Order = Total Revenue ÷ Total Orders**

## 🧰 Excel Functions
`SUM` → total values  
`SUMIF` → conditional total  
`COUNTIF` → conditional count  
`AVERAGE` → mean  
`AVERAGEIF` → conditional mean

## 📊 Pivot Tables
Use Rows for dimensions.
Use Values for measures.
Try Month, Category, Channel and Region.
Compare Orders, Units and Revenue.

## 🧠 Business Thinking
Ask what the number means.
Ask which dimension is being compared.
Ask which measure is being used.
Ask what business question the result answers.

## 🧹 Data Quality
Check missing values.
Check numeric fields.
Check labels.
Check duplicate or inconsistent records.

## 🔄 Workflow
1. Load data.
2. Inspect data.
3. Identify dimensions.
4. Identify measures.
5. Aggregate.
6. Compare.
7. Visualise.
8. Interpret.
9. Record insights.

## ⚠️ Avoid
Do not invent reasons unsupported by the data.
Do not rely on a chart without checking its source table.
Do not confuse orders with units.
Do not treat sample data as real company data.
