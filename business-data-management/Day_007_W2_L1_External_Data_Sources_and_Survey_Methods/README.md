---

## 📚 Topics Covered

### 🔹 1. External Data Sources

Business analysis may require data collected from sources outside the organisation.

Examples:

- Government datasets
- Public databases
- Company reports
- Customer surveys
- Interviews
- Observations

---

### 🔹 2. Primary Data

**Primary data** is collected directly for a specific purpose.

Examples:

- Surveys
- Interviews
- Observations
- Questionnaires

---

### 🔹 3. Secondary Data

**Secondary data** has already been collected or published by another source.

Examples:

- Government statistics
- Research reports
- Public datasets
- Company reports

---

## 📝 Survey Methods

A survey collects information from respondents using structured questions.

Common response types:

- Multiple choice
- Numeric response
- Rating scale
- Open-ended response

## 🧠 Key Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Primary Data | Data collected directly |
| Secondary Data | Existing data collected by another source |
| Survey | Structured method of collecting responses |
| Respondent | Person providing survey information |
| External Data | Data obtained from outside the organisation |
