## 🔹 1. External Data Sources

External data is information obtained from sources outside an organisation.

Examples:

- Government datasets
- Public databases
- Research reports
- Company reports
- Customer surveys

---

## 🔹 2. Primary Data

**Primary data** is collected directly for a specific purpose.

Examples:

- Surveys
- Interviews
- Questionnaires
- Observation

**Example:** A company asks customers about their satisfaction.

---

## 🔹 3. Secondary Data

**Secondary data** is data that has already been collected or published by another source.

Examples:

- Government statistics
- Public datasets
- Research reports
- Company reports

---

## 🔹 4. Survey

A **survey** is a structured method of collecting information from respondents.

A survey may ask about:

- Demographics
- Preferences
- Purchase behaviour
- Spending
- Satisfaction

---

## 🔹 5. Respondent

A **respondent** is the person who provides answers to a survey.

Example:

**Survey → Questions → Respondent → Responses → Dataset**

---

## 🔹 6. Survey Response Types

Common response types include:

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Multiple Choice | Male / Female |
| Numeric | Monthly Spend |
| Rating | Satisfaction 1–5 |
| Open-ended | Reason for purchase |

## 🎯 Key Takeaways

- External data comes from outside the organisation.
- Primary data is collected directly.
- Secondary data already exists.
- Surveys are an important data-collection method.
- Survey responses can be converted into a dataset for business analysis.
