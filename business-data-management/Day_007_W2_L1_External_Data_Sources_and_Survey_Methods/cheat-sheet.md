## 🌐 External Data

**External Data** → Data obtained from outside an organisation.

Examples:

- Government datasets
- Public databases
- Research reports
- Company reports
- Customer surveys

---

## 📥 Primary vs Secondary Data

| Type | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Data** | Collected directly | Survey, Interview, Observation |
| **Secondary Data** | Already collected/published | Government data, Reports, Public datasets |

---

## 📝 Survey

**Survey = Structured method of collecting information from respondents.**

Common information:

- Demographics
- Preferences
- Purchase behaviour
- Spending
- Satisfaction

---

## 👤 Respondent

**Respondent** = Person who provides information in a survey.

**Survey → Questions → Respondent → Responses → Dataset**

---

## 📊 Response Types

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Multiple Choice | City / Gender |
| Numeric | Monthly Spend |
| Rating | Satisfaction 1–5 |
| Open-ended | Reason for purchase |

---

## 💼 Business Data Flow

**Data Source → Data Collection → Dataset → Analysis → Business Insight**

---

## 🧠 Remember

**Primary = Collect yourself**

**Secondary = Already available**

**Survey = Collect responses**

**Respondent = Gives responses**

**External Data = Comes from outside**
