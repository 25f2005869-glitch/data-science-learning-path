# 📊 Day 041 — Introduction to the Dataset: Sales, Production and Inventory

**Author:** Saloni Tiwari  
**Programme:** IIT Madras BS Degree — Diploma Level  
**Day:** 041  
**Topic:** W7_L4 Introduction to the Dataset — Sales, Production and Inventory

> This day focuses on understanding the business dataset before performing detailed revenue and operational analysis.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand the structure of a business dataset.
- Identify sales-related variables.
- Identify production-related variables.
- Identify inventory-related variables.
- Understand product and regional dimensions.
- Prepare data for the next stages of analysis.

## 💰 Sales View

The Sales View focuses on:

- Product
- Region
- Customer type
- Sales units
- Revenue

This view helps understand business sales performance.

## 🏭 Production View

The Production View focuses on:

- Production units
- Scrap units
- Production cost
- Product
- Region

This view helps understand manufacturing activity.

## 📦 Inventory View

The Inventory View focuses on:

- Closing inventory
- Product
- Region
- Month

Inventory information provides operational context.

## 📅 Monthly View

The Monthly View allows observations to be studied over time.

Monthly analysis can later support:

- Revenue trends
- Sales trends
- Production trends
- Inventory analysis

## 📖 Data Dictionary

The Data Dictionary explains each important field.

Always review the Data Dictionary before analysing an unfamiliar dataset.

## 📈 Dashboard

The dashboard provides visual summaries of:

- Sales vs Production
- Closing Inventory
- Monthly Revenue

The charts are designed for practical business interpretation.

## 🧠 Business Questions

Important questions include:

1. What products are present?
2. Which regions are present?
3. What is the total sales volume?
4. What is total production?
5. How much inventory is available?
6. Which product has the highest revenue?
7. How does revenue change by month?
8. How do sales and production compare?
