## 💰 Revenue

Revenue = Sales value generated.

## 🏭 Production

Production Units = Units manufactured.

## 🛒 Sales

Sales Units = Units sold.

## 📦 Inventory

Closing Inventory = Stock remaining at period end.

## ♻️ Scrap

Scrap Units = Units classified as scrap.

## 💵 Cost

Production Cost = Cost associated with production.

## 📈 Gross Margin

Gross Margin = Revenue - Production Cost

## 🔄 Inventory Relationship

Production + Opening Inventory - Sales = Closing Inventory

This is a simplified conceptual relationship.

## 📅 Time Analysis

Group data by Month.

Then calculate:

- Revenue
- Sales
- Production
- Inventory
