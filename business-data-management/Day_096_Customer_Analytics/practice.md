# 14. Repeat Customer Practice

Using `Customer_ID`, identify customers who have more than one order.

### Tasks

1. Count orders for each customer.
2. Identify customers with more than one order.
3. Mark them as `Yes` or `No`.
4. Calculate the number of repeat customers.
5. Calculate Repeat Customer Rate.

### Formula

**Repeat Customer Rate = Repeat Customers ÷ Total Customers**
