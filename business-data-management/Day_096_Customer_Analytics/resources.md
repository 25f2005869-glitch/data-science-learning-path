# 5. Customer Spending Analysis

Study customer-level spending patterns.

### Important Metrics

- Total Revenue
- Average Customer Spend
- Average Order Value
- Orders per Customer
- Repeat Customers
- Repeat Customer Rate

### Key Formulas

**Average Customer Spend**

Total Revenue ÷ Total Customers

**Average Order Value**

Total Revenue ÷ Total Orders

**Repeat Customer Rate**

Repeat Customers ÷ Total Customers

# 11. Customer KPIs

Study the meaning and calculation of:

| KPI | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Total Customers | Unique customer count |
| Active Customers | Unique active customers |
| Total Revenue | Total revenue |
| Average Customer Spend | Revenue per customer |
| Average Order Value | Revenue per order |
| Total Orders | Number of transactions |
| Average Orders per Customer | Orders per customer |
| Repeat Customers | Customers with multiple orders |
| Repeat Customer Rate | Repeat customers as a share of customers |
| Average Satisfaction | Average satisfaction score |

# 15. Business Insight Resources

Learn to convert calculations into observations.

A good business insight should contain:

**Metric → Observation → Possible Area for Further Investigation**

Example structure:

> Revenue is distributed differently across customer segments. Further analysis can examine order frequency and customer spending patterns within each segment.

Avoid writing conclusions that are not supported by the dataset.
