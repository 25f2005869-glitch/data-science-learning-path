# 📚 Day 040 Resources — Background Context to the Case

**Author:** Saloni Tiwari  
**Programme:** IIT Madras BS Degree — Diploma Level  
**Day:** 040  
**Topic:** Background Context to the Case

## 🎓 Learning Focus

Day 040 is about business background and case context.

The goal is to understand the analytical setting before detailed calculations.

## 📖 Workbook Resources

Background_Context provides the case setting.

Business_Setting explains the major business areas.

Stakeholders lists possible information needs.

Business_Questions converts context into analytical questions.

Data_Dictionary explains fields.

Analysis_Roadmap gives the workflow.

Context_Notes records scope and data-use notes.

Product_Context_Summary provides a product view.

Region_Context_Summary provides a regional view.

Background_Dashboard provides illustrative charts.

## 🧰 Excel Skills

Read structured tables.

Use SUMIF for category summaries.

Review grouped financial measures.

Interpret charts.

## 🐍 Python Skills

Read Excel data with pandas.

Use groupby for summaries.

Create CSV outputs.

Create basic matplotlib charts.

## 🧭 Recommended Sequence

1. Read Background_Context.
2. Read Business_Setting.
3. Review Stakeholders.
4. Read Data_Dictionary.
5. Inspect raw data.
6. Review summaries.
7. Attempt questions.
8. Review dashboard.
9. Complete practice.

## 🔎 Revision Questions

What is the business sector?

What products are included?

What regions are included?

What customer types are included?

What are the operational measures?

What are the financial measures?

Who may use the analysis?
