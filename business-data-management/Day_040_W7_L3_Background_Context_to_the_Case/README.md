## 🚀 Day Overview

Day 040 develops the background context for the ACE Gears manufacturing case.

The focus is understanding the business setting before detailed dataset analysis.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand the manufacturing case setting.
- Identify the major business dimensions.
- Understand the role of products and regions.
- Understand customer groups.
- Identify operational measures.
- Identify financial measures.
- Identify possible stakeholders.
- Prepare for detailed case analysis.

## 🏭 Business Background

ACE Gears is treated as a manufacturing-sector business case for BDM practice.

The case can be viewed through product, region, customer, operations and finance.

## 📦 Products

The illustrative workbook contains Gears, Shafts and Bearings.

Product is an important dimension for comparing business performance.

## 🌍 Regions

The illustrative workbook contains North, South and West.

Regional analysis allows performance to be compared across business areas.

## 👥 Customers

The sample contains OEM and Distributor customer types.

Customer type can be used to organize sales and revenue analysis.

## ⚙️ Operations

Production Units represent recorded output.

Sales Units represent recorded units sold.

Scrap Units represent units recorded as scrap.

## 💰 Finance

Revenue represents recorded sales value.

Production Cost represents recorded production expenditure.

Gross Margin is calculated as Revenue minus Production Cost.

## 🧑‍💼 Stakeholders

Management, sales, production, finance and operations can have different information needs.

## 📊 Business Questions

The background context leads to questions about products, regions, customers, operations and financial performance.

## 🧭 Analysis Roadmap

Understand context → inspect data → summarize dimensions → calculate KPIs → visualize → investigate deeper.
