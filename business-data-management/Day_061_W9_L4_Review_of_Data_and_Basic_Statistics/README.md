# 📊 Day 061 — Review of Data and Basic Statistics

## 📌 Day Overview

Day 061 — W9_L4 focuses on reviewing a business dataset before deeper analysis.
The package introduces descriptive statistics using the internal sourcing dataset from the previous day.
The dataset is illustrative and is not official IIT Madras or company data.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

1. Review raw business data.
2. Check missing values and duplicates.
3. Identify categorical and numerical variables.
4. Calculate mean, median and standard deviation.
5. Calculate minimum, maximum and range.
6. Understand quartiles and IQR.
7. Compare summary statistics across business groups.
8. Interpret descriptive statistics carefully.

## 🔍 Data Review

Start with the raw dataset.
Check record counts.
Check missing values.
Check duplicate request IDs.
Review category values.
Review numerical ranges.
Confirm that units and definitions are understood.

## 📐 Basic Statistics

Mean is the arithmetic average.
Median is the middle value after sorting.
Mode is the most frequent value.
Minimum is the smallest observed value.
Maximum is the largest observed value.
Range = Maximum − Minimum.
Standard deviation describes dispersion around the mean.
Q1 is the 25th percentile.
Q3 is the 75th percentile.
IQR = Q3 − Q1.

## ⚠️ Interpretation

An average is a summary, not a complete description of a distribution.
Mean and median can differ when observations are unevenly distributed.
Standard deviation measures dispersion but does not explain its cause.
Correlation does not establish causation.
Results from this dataset should not be presented as real organizational evidence.
