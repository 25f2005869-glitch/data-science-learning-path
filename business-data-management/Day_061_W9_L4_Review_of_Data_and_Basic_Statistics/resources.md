## Data Quality Checklist

Check record count.
Check identifiers.
Check duplicates.
Check missing values.
Check category consistency.
Check numerical ranges.
Check date or month consistency.
Check unit definitions.
Check derived columns.

## Analysis Checklist

Define the question.
Identify the relevant variables.
Review data quality.
Calculate descriptive statistics.
Segment where useful.
Compare multiple statistics.
Visualize.
Interpret with context.

## Responsible Analytics

Do not hide missing data.
Do not treat a small illustrative dataset as an industry benchmark.
Do not claim causation from descriptive statistics.
Document metric definitions.
Protect confidential business information.
