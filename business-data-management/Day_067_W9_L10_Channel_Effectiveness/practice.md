## Mini Case

A business uses multiple sourcing channels.

Management wants to understand channel effectiveness.

Build a scorecard with five dimensions.

Use reach.

Use conversion.

Use cost.

Use speed.

Use satisfaction.

Do not collapse the five dimensions into one arbitrary score.

## Analysis Questions

Which channel has the largest candidate count?

Which channel has the highest selection rate?

Which channel has the lowest average sourcing cost?

Which channel has the shortest average days to fill?

How does satisfaction vary?

Does the same channel lead on every metric?

## Data Quality Questions

Are candidate IDs unique?

Are selection flags binary?

Are costs numeric?

Are days positive?

Are satisfaction scores within the expected scale?
