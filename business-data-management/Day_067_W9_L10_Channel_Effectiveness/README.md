# 📊 Day 067 — Channel Effectiveness

## 📌 Topic

Day 067 studies how different sourcing channels perform using measurable business outcomes.

## 🎯 Objectives

1. Understand channel effectiveness.
2. Measure sourcing reach.
3. Measure selection conversion.
4. Compare sourcing cost.
5. Compare time to fill.
6. Include satisfaction as a quality signal.
7. Segment channel performance.
8. Interpret multiple KPIs together.

## 🧩 Business Question

Which sourcing channels are generating candidates, selections, speed, and cost outcomes in the illustrative dataset?

## 📁 Dataset

The workbook contains 30 illustrative sourcing records.

The data covers January to March 2023.

Departments include Technology, Finance, HR, Sales, and Operations.

Regions include North, South, and West.

## 🔎 Channels

Internal Transfer represents movement across existing roles.

Internal Promotion represents movement into a higher role.

Employee Referral represents employee-referred candidates.

Job Portal represents external portal sourcing.

Recruitment Agency represents agency-assisted sourcing.

## 📐 Core Metrics

Candidate Count measures records represented by a channel.

Application Count measures recorded applications.

Selected Count measures records with Selected_Flag equal to one.

Selection Rate equals selected candidates divided by candidates.

Average Days to Fill measures hiring speed.

Average Sourcing Cost measures average cost per record.

Total Sourcing Cost measures channel spending in the dataset.

Average Satisfaction adds a service-quality dimension.

Cost per Selected divides total sourcing cost by selected records.

## 📊 Dashboard

Candidates by Sourcing Channel.

Selection Rate by Sourcing Channel.

Average Sourcing Cost by Channel.

Average Days to Fill by Channel.

## 🧮 Analysis Flow

Inspect the raw data.

Understand channel definitions.

Calculate channel KPIs.

Compare departments.

Compare regions.

Review monthly movement.

Review role-level variation.

Run the Python analysis.

## 🐍 Python

The script reads the workbook and calculates channel, department, region, monthly, and role summaries.

It also creates four charts.

## ⚠️ Data Note

The dataset is synthetic and created for learning.

It is not official IIT Madras data.

It is not company recruitment data.

The metrics should not be interpreted as real labor-market evidence.

## 💡 Interpretation

High candidate volume measures reach, not necessarily quality.

A high selection rate does not by itself establish lower total cost.

A lower average time to fill should be considered with cost and conversion.

Satisfaction can provide additional context.
