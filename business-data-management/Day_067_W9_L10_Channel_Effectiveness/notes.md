# 📝 Day 067 Notes — Channel Effectiveness

## 1. Meaning

Channel effectiveness describes how a sourcing channel performs against defined business measures.

## 2. Reach

Candidate count provides a simple measure of channel reach in the dataset.

## 3. Applications

Application count measures recorded application activity.

## 4. Selection

Selected_Flag records whether the candidate record resulted in selection.

## 5. Selection Rate

Selection Rate = Selected Count / Candidate Count × 100.

## 6. Cost

Sourcing cost measures the illustrative resource cost associated with each record.

## 7. Average Cost

Average Sourcing Cost = Total Sourcing Cost / Candidate Count.

## 8. Total Cost

Total Sourcing Cost is the sum of channel-level sourcing costs.

## 9. Cost per Selected

Cost per Selected = Total Sourcing Cost / Selected Count.

## 10. Speed

Days_to_Fill measures the number of days associated with filling the role.

## 11. Average Speed

Average Days to Fill is the arithmetic mean of Days_to_Fill.

## 12. Satisfaction

Satisfaction_Score is a numeric quality indicator in the illustrative dataset.

## 13. Multi-KPI Analysis

Channel effectiveness should be examined across several measures.

## 14. Volume Limitation

Large candidate volume does not automatically imply strong conversion.

## 15. Conversion Limitation

Selection rate can be affected by role mix and candidate mix.

## 16. Cost Limitation

Average cost hides the distribution of individual sourcing costs.

## 17. Speed Limitation

Average days can hide unusually long or short cases.

## 18. Satisfaction Limitation

Satisfaction is illustrative and does not represent a validated survey instrument.

## 19. Department Segmentation

Department analysis shows how sourcing activity differs across functions.

## 20. Region Segmentation

Region analysis provides geographic grouping.

## 21. Monthly Analysis

Monthly grouping helps identify changes across the observation period.

## 22. Role Analysis

Role grouping helps reveal whether channel results vary by target role.

## 23. Internal Channels

Internal channels may represent existing workforce movement.

## 24. Referral Channel

Employee referrals provide another sourcing route.

## 25. Portal Channel

Job portals represent external digital sourcing.

## 26. Agency Channel

Recruitment agencies represent an external intermediary.

## 27. Channel Comparison

Comparisons should use the same definitions and denominators.

## 28. Denominator

Selection rate uses candidate records as the denominator in this workbook.

## 29. Cost Denominator

Cost per selected uses selected records as the denominator.

## 30. Data Quality

Check missing values before calculating KPIs.

## 31. Duplicate Records

Candidate identifiers should be reviewed for duplicate observations.

## 32. Date Consistency

Month values should follow a consistent format.

## 33. Numeric Fields

Scores, costs, days, applications, and flags should be numeric.

## 34. Binary Flag

Selected_Flag uses zero and one in the learning dataset.

## 35. Dashboard

The dashboard provides a compact comparison of four channel metrics.

## 36. Python

Pandas is used for aggregation.

Matplotlib is used for charts.

## 37. Business Interpretation

No single metric fully describes channel effectiveness.

## 38. Recommended Reading Pattern

Start with volume.

Move to conversion.

Then inspect cost.

Then inspect speed.

Then inspect satisfaction.

## 39. Final Note

The purpose is to practice structured business analysis rather than create a real recruitment recommendation.
