## 📌 Fixed Deposit Analysis

A Fixed Deposit is a financial product where money is deposited for a specified period.

For analysis, we can study:

- Existing FD holders
- FD amount
- People planning to invest
- Potential FD customers

---

## 📊 FD Rate

**FD Rate = FD Customers / Total Respondents**

A sample rate can be applied to a larger target population for an illustrative market estimate.

---

## 💰 FD Market

**Potential FD Customers = Target Population × Potential FD Rate**

**Potential FD Market = Potential FD Customers × Average FD Amount**

---

## 🏦 Borrowing Analysis

Borrowing analysis studies:

- Number of borrowers
- Borrowing rate
- Borrowing amount
- Borrowing purpose

---

## 🧮 Borrowing Rate

**Borrowing Rate = Number of Borrowers / Total Respondents**

---

## 💵 Borrowing Market

**Potential Borrowers = Target Population × Borrowing Rate**

**Potential Borrowing Market = Potential Borrowers × Average Borrowing Amount**

---

## 🎯 Borrowing Purposes

The dataset includes purposes such as:

- Home
- Business
- Education
- Personal

---

## 💡 Main Learning

Financial survey data can be converted into market estimates by using:

**Sample → Rate → Target Population → Estimated Customers → Market Value**
