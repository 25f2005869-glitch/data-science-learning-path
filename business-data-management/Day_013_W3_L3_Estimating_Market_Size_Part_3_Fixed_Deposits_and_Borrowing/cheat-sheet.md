## 🧮 Core Formulas

### FD Rate

`FD Customers / Total Respondents`

### Potential FD Customers

`Target Population × Potential FD Rate`

### Average FD Amount

`AVERAGEIF()`

### Potential FD Market

`Potential FD Customers × Average FD Amount`

---

## 🏦 Borrowing

### Borrowing Rate

`Borrowers / Total Respondents`

### Potential Borrowers

`Target Population × Borrowing Rate`

### Average Borrowing

`AVERAGEIF()`

### Potential Borrowing Market

`Potential Borrowers × Average Borrowing Amount`
