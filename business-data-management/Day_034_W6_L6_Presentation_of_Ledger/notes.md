## 📚 Lecture

W6_L6 focuses on Presentation of Ledger.

The topic continues the inventory analysis sequence.

The main objective is to present inventory movements clearly.

## 📦 Inventory Ledger

An inventory ledger records inventory movements.

It provides transaction-level information.

Each record describes a movement or stock position.

The ledger becomes the source for summary analysis.

## 📅 Date

Date identifies when an inventory movement is recorded.

Dates allow the ledger to be arranged chronologically.

They also allow monthly analysis.

Date consistency is important for reporting.

## 🏷️ Product

Product identifies the inventory item.

Product-level grouping allows comparison.

Examples in the practical dataset include:

- Laptop
- Smartphone
- Shoes

## 🔄 Transaction Type

Transaction_Type identifies the type of movement.

The practical dataset uses:

- Opening
- Purchase
- Sale

Purchases represent incoming inventory.

Sales represent outgoing inventory.

## 📥 Purchases

Purchases increase available inventory.

Total purchases can be calculated by product.

They can also be summarized by month.

Purchase information helps explain stock movements.

## 📤 Sales

Sales represent units leaving inventory.

Total sales units can be calculated by product.

Sales can also be summarized by month.

Sales volume is important when interpreting inventory.

## 📊 Opening Stock

Opening stock represents inventory before the recorded movement.

It provides the starting point for the transaction.

Opening stock should be checked when reviewing a ledger.

## 📊 Closing Stock

Closing stock represents the stock position after the recorded movement.

It is important for inventory reporting.

Changes in closing stock can be presented over time.

## 📈 Product-Level Summary

Product_Ledger_Summary groups detailed records by product.

Important measures include:

- Total purchases
- Total sales units
- Average inventory
- Closing stock
- Net stock change

## 📅 Monthly Summary

Monthly_Ledger groups records by month.

The summary includes:

- Purchases
- Sales units
- Closing stock

This provides a time-based view of inventory activity.

## 📊 Transaction Summary

Transaction_Summary groups records by transaction type.

It shows:

- Number of records
- Purchases
- Sales units

This helps explain the composition of ledger activity.

## 🎯 Presentation

Presentation means converting detailed ledger information into a form that is easier to understand.

Raw data is useful for verification.

Summaries are useful for communication.

Charts are useful for visual comparison.

## 📊 Dashboard

The dashboard contains headline inventory information.

It presents sales units by product.

It presents monthly closing stock.

It also displays basic KPIs.

## 🎨 Chart Design

Charts should have clear titles.

Axes should have meaningful labels.

Units should be visible.

Colours should be consistent.

The chart should match the question being answered.

## 🔎 Data Quality

Check missing dates.

Check duplicate records.

Check product names.

Check transaction types.

Check numeric values.

Check chronological order.

Check stock movement logic.

## 🧠 Business Interpretation

A purchase adds inventory.

A sale reduces inventory.

Closing stock reflects the recorded stock position.

High sales can affect inventory availability.

The ledger shows what was recorded.

Additional business context may be required to explain why something happened.

## 📝 Presentation Sequence

1. State the business question.
2. Explain the ledger.
3. Explain fields.
4. Present product summary.
5. Present monthly summary.
6. Show charts.
7. Explain observations.
8. State limitations.

## ⚠️ Common Errors

Do not confuse purchases with sales.

Do not ignore the date.

Do not mix products.

Do not remove units from charts.

Do not make unsupported causal claims.

## 🔁 Revision

Remember:

Ledger = detailed movement record.

Product summary = product-level view.

Monthly summary = time-based view.

Dashboard = presentation layer.

## 🎯 Final Learning

The central idea of W6_L6 is that detailed ledger data should be converted into clear, evidence-based business presentation.
