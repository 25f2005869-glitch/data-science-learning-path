## 🧮 Core Formulas

### Purchase Rate

`=Potential_Buyers / Total_Respondents`

---

### Estimated Buyers

`=Target_Population * Purchase_Rate`

---

### Average Price

`=AVERAGE(Price_Range)`

---

### Average Loan

`=AVERAGEIF(Loan_Interest_Range,"Yes",Loan_Amount_Range)`

---

## 📊 Useful Excel Functions

### COUNTIF

`=COUNTIF(Range,"Yes")`

Count respondents matching one condition.

### COUNTIFS

`=COUNTIFS(Region_Range,"North",Planning_Range,"Yes")`

Count using multiple conditions.

### AVERAGE

`=AVERAGE(Range)`

Calculate an average.

### AVERAGEIF

`=AVERAGEIF(Criteria_Range,"Yes",Average_Range)`

Calculate an average based on one condition.

### AVERAGEIFS

`=AVERAGEIFS(Average_Range,Criteria_Range,Criteria)`

Calculate an average using multiple conditions.

### SUM

`=SUM(Range)`

Calculate a total.

## 💰 Market Value

**Estimated Market Value**

`=Estimated Buyers × Average Product Price`

**Potential Loan Market**

`=Estimated Buyers × Average Loan Amount`
