## 📌 What is Market Size?

Market size is an estimate of the potential number or value of customers/business opportunities for a product or service.

Here, the focus is on the potential **two-wheeler and two-wheeler loan market**.

---

## 📊 Survey-Based Estimation

A survey gives information about a sample of people.

For example:

- Total respondents
- People planning to buy
- People interested in loans
- Expected bike price
- Expected loan amount

The sample information can then be used to estimate a larger target population.

---

## 🧮 Purchase Rate

Purchase Rate = Number Planning to Buy / Total Respondents

Example:

If 6 out of 15 respondents plan to buy:

Purchase Rate = 6 / 15 = 40%

---

## 📈 Market Estimation

Estimated Buyers = Target Population × Purchase Rate

The result is an **estimated potential market**, not an exact count.

---

## 💰 Loan Analysis

Important variables:

- Loan interest
- Loan amount
- Bike price
- Annual income

These can help understand potential financing demand.

---

## 🌍 Regional Analysis

Market demand can also be compared across regions.

For each region, calculate:

- Number of respondents
- Number planning to buy
- Buy rate
- Average loan amount

---

## 💡 Main Learning

The important process is:

**Survey → Rate → Population Estimate → Market Size → Business Insight**
