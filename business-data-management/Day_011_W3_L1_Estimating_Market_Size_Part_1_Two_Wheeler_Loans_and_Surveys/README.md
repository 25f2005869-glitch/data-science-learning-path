## 🧮 Basic Market Estimation

A simple estimation approach:

**Sample Purchase Rate**

Purchase Rate = Potential Buyers in Sample / Total Sample

Then:

**Estimated Market Size**

Estimated Market Size = Target Population × Purchase Rate

---

## 💰 Market Value

A potential market value can be estimated using:

**Market Value = Estimated Buyers × Average Product Price**

For a loan-focused analysis, the potential loan market can be estimated using:

**Loan Market = Estimated Buyers × Average Loan Amount**
