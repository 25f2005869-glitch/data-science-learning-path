## 📌 Overview
This practical focuses on analysing manufacturing scrap costs and understanding their impact on profitability using Excel and Python.

*Note: The dataset contains illustrative educational data.*

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Understand scrap costs and scrap rates.
- Calculate unit production cost.
- Analyse revenue, gross profit, and margins.
- Compare product and regional performance.
- Create charts and present business insights.

## 🧮 Key Formulas
- **Scrap Rate (%)** = Scrap Units / Production Units × 100
- **Unit Production Cost** = Material Cost + Labour Cost + Overhead Cost
- **Scrap Cost** = Scrap Units × Unit Production Cost
- **Revenue** = Sales Units × Selling Price per Unit
- **Gross Profit** = Revenue − Cost of Sales
- **Gross Margin (%)** = Gross Profit / Revenue × 100
