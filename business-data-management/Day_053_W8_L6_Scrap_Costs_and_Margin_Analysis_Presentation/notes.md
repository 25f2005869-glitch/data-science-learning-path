# Day 053 — Detailed Notes

**Author:** Saloni Tiwari  
**Programme:** IIT Madras BS Degree — Diploma Level  
**Day:** 053  
**Topic:** Scrap Costs and Margin Analysis Presentation

## 📖 Overview

Day 053 develops a presentation-ready analysis connecting operational scrap with cost and margin metrics.

The workbook uses an illustrative ACE Gears-style manufacturing dataset.

It is not official IIT Madras data and is not actual company performance.

## 1️⃣ Scrap

Scrap represents produced units that are classified as unusable or rejected in the illustrative dataset.

## 2️⃣ Scrap Rate

Scrap Rate measures scrap relative to production volume.

`Scrap Rate = Scrap Units ÷ Production Units`

## 3️⃣ Scrap Cost

Scrap Cost estimates the modeled production cost attached to scrap units.

## 4️⃣ Revenue

Revenue is based on sold units and selling price.

## 5️⃣ Cost of Sales

Cost of Sales uses the modeled unit production cost for sold units.

## 6️⃣ Gross Profit

Gross Profit is revenue less modeled cost of sales.

## 7️⃣ Margin

Margin expresses gross profit relative to revenue.

## 8️⃣ Margin Impact

A presentation can place scrap and margin metrics together so operational loss can be discussed alongside profitability.

## 9️⃣ Product View

Compare scrap units, scrap rate, scrap cost and margin by product.

## 🔟 Monthly View

Use the monthly table to identify changes over the six-month illustrative period.

## 🌍 Regional View

Use regional aggregation to compare operating patterns across regions.

## ⚠️ Caution

Real-world scrap accounting can depend on company-specific costing policies. Validate definitions before using the analysis operationally.
