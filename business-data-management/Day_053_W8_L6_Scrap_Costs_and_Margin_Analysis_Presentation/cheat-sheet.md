**Topic:** Scrap Costs and Margin Analysis Presentation

## ⚡ Formulas

`Scrap Rate = Scrap ÷ Production`

`Unit Production Cost = Material + Labour + Overhead`

`Scrap Cost = Scrap × Unit Production Cost`

`Revenue = Sales × Price`

`Gross Profit = Revenue − Cost of Sales`

`Margin = Gross Profit ÷ Revenue`

## 📌 Presentation Order

Context → Production → Scrap → Scrap Rate → Scrap Cost → Revenue → Gross Profit → Margin → Product/Month/Region → Interpretation
