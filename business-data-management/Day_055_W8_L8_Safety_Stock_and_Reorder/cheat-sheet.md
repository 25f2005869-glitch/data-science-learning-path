# ⚡ Day 055 Cheat Sheet

## 🎯 Topic

Safety Stock and Reorder

## 🔑 Important Terms

### Average Daily Demand

Average demand per day.

### Lead Time

Time required for replenishment.

### Safety Stock

Extra inventory maintained as protection.

### Lead-Time Demand

Expected demand during lead time.

### Reorder Point

Inventory level used to trigger replenishment.

### Closing Inventory

Inventory available at the end of a period.

### Inventory Gap

Difference between closing inventory and reorder point.

## 🧮 Formula 1

Lead-Time Demand

Average Daily Demand × Average Lead Time

## 🧮 Formula 2

Reorder Point

Lead-Time Demand + Safety Stock

## 🧮 Formula 3

Reorder Coverage Days

Reorder Point ÷ Average Daily Demand

## 🧮 Formula 4

Inventory Gap

Closing Inventory − Reorder Point

## 📌 Interpretation

Negative Inventory Gap

→ Below reorder point

Zero Inventory Gap

→ At reorder point

Positive Inventory Gap

→ Above reorder point
