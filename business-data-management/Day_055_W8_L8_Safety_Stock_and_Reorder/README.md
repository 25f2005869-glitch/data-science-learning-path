# 📦 Day 055 — Safety Stock and Reorder

## 🎯 Learning Objective

Understand safety stock and reorder point calculations.

Learn how inventory data can be used to identify stock requirements.

## 📚 Topic

**W8_L8 — Safety Stock and Reorder**

This day continues the inventory analysis work from the previous lectures.

The focus is on calculating inventory protection levels and reorder requirements.

## 🧠 Key Concepts

- Safety Stock
- Lead-Time Demand
- Reorder Point
- Average Daily Demand
- Average Lead Time
- Reorder Coverage Days
- Closing Inventory
- Inventory Gap
- Stock Status

## 🧮 Core Formula

Lead-Time Demand:

Average Daily Demand × Average Lead Time

## 📌 Reorder Point

Reorder Point is calculated as:

Lead-Time Demand + Safety Stock

## 📊 Inventory Gap

Inventory Gap is:

Closing Inventory − Reorder Point

A negative value indicates inventory is below the reorder point.

## 🔍 Business Interpretation

A business can use reorder point analysis to understand when inventory needs replenishment.

Safety stock provides additional inventory protection against uncertainty.

Lead-time demand represents expected demand during the replenishment period.
