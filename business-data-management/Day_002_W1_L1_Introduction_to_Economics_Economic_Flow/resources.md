## 🔄 Key Concept

The central concept for Day 002 is the relationship between:

**Economic Activity → Production → Income → Consumption → Expenditure**

This relationship helps explain how economic activities are interconnected.

---

## 🏠 Economic Participants

The main participants discussed for understanding economic activity include:

- Households
- Firms
- Government

---

## 💡 Study Focus

While watching the lecture, focus on understanding:

1. What Economics studies
2. Who participates in economic activities
3. What households do in an economy
4. What firms do in an economy
5. What factors of production are
6. How goods and services move
7. How income moves
8. How expenditure takes place
9. How real flow differs from money flow
10. How economic activities form a circular flow
