## 🎯 Core Idea

**Economics** studies economic activities and the interactions between different participants in an economy.

**Economic Flow** explains how **factors of production, goods, services, income, and expenditure** move between economic participants.

---

## 👥 Major Economic Participants

### 🏠 Households

- Provide factors of production
- Earn income
- Purchase goods and services
- Spend money on consumption

### 🏢 Firms

- Use factors of production
- Produce goods and services
- Pay income
- Sell goods and services

### 🏛️ Government

- Collects taxes
- Makes expenditures
- Provides public services
- Participates in economic activity

---

## 🏭 Factors of Production

The major factors of production are:

- **Land**
- **Labour**
- **Capital**
- **Entrepreneurship**

---

## 🔄 Household–Firm Flow

### Households → Firms

**Factors of Production**

### Firms → Households

**Income**

### Firms → Households

**Goods & Services**

### Households → Firms

**Consumption Expenditure**

---

## 💧 Two Important Flows

### Real Flow

Movement of:

- Goods
- Services
- Factors of production

### Money Flow

Movement of:

- Income
- Payments
- Expenditure

---

## 📊 Circular Economic Flow

**Households → Factors of Production → Firms**

**Firms → Income → Households**

**Firms → Goods & Services → Households**

**Households → Expenditure → Firms**

---

## 💰 Important Relationships

**Production → Income**

**Income → Consumption**

**Consumption → Expenditure**

**Expenditure → Revenue**

**Revenue → Business Activity**

---

## 📈 Economic Flow and Data

Economic activities generate data.

Examples:

| Activity | Possible Data |
|---|---|
| Production | Production quantity |
| Sales | Quantity sold, revenue |
| Employment | Number of employees, wages |
| Consumption | Household expenditure |
| Finance | Income, costs, payments |
| Government | Taxes, expenditure |

---

## 🧠 Important Terms

| Term | Quick Meaning |
|---|---|
| Economics | Study of economic activities and interactions |
| Economic Agent | Participant in economic activity |
| Household | Economic unit that can provide resources and consume goods/services |
| Firm | Organisation that produces goods/services |
| Factor of Production | Resource used for production |
| Income | Earnings received |
| Expenditure | Money spent |
| Real Flow | Flow of goods, services and factors |
| Money Flow | Flow of income, payments and expenditure |

---

## ⚡ Quick Comparison

| Real Flow | Money Flow |
|---|---|
| Goods | Payments |
| Services | Income |
| Factors of production | Expenditure |
| Physical/economic resources | Monetary transactions |

---

## 🎯 Remember

- Households can provide **factors of production**.
- Firms use factors of production to create **goods and services**.
- Firms provide **income** to households.
- Households spend income on **goods and services**.
- Household spending becomes **expenditure**.
- Expenditure contributes to business **revenue**.
- Real flow and money flow are interconnected.
