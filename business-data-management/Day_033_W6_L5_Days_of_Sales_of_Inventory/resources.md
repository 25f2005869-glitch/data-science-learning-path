## 🧮 Formula Resource

Average Daily Sales:

`Sales Units / Observation Days`

DSI:

`Average Inventory / Average Daily Sales`

Inventory Turnover:

`Sales Units / Average Inventory`

## 🧠 Concept Resource

DSI converts the relationship between inventory and sales velocity into days.

This makes inventory coverage easier to compare across products when the methodology is consistent.
