## 💼 Business Case

Imagine an online retailer has three products.

One product has a relatively high DSI.

Another product has a relatively low DSI.

Do not immediately call either product good or bad.

Instead examine:

- Sales velocity
- Inventory level
- Demand pattern
- Replenishment policy
- Observation period
