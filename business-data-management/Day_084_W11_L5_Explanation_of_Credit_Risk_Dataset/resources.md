## 6. Data Dictionary Practice
For each field ask:
- What does it measure?
- What unit does it use?
- Is it raw or derived?
- Is it categorical or numeric?
- When is it known?
- Could it leak the target?

## 7. Quality Checklist
Check:
- Row count.
- Duplicate IDs.
- Missing values.
- Data types.
- Category consistency.
- Numeric ranges.
- Flag values.
- Outliers.
- Target balance.
- Leakage.

## 8. Business Interpretation
Do not stop at a percentage.
Ask about denominator, exposure, income, DTI, score, sample size and business process.
