# 📝 Day 037 Notes — Course Project Instructions & Guidelines

**Author:** Saloni Tiwari  
**Programme:** IIT Madras BS Degree — Diploma Level  
**Day:** 037  
**Topic:** Course Project Instructions, Guidelines & Workflow

## 1. Project Purpose

The course project brings together business-data skills learned during BDM.

The focus is not only on calculations.

The analysis should answer meaningful business questions.

## 2. Start With the Problem

Define the business context.

Identify the decision or question that the analysis supports.

A good problem statement gives direction to the project.

## 3. Understand the Data

Identify rows and columns.

Understand important fields.

Identify identifiers and measures.

Identify categorical and numerical variables.

## 4. Data Dictionary

A data dictionary records field names and meanings.

It helps prevent incorrect interpretation.

It also improves communication with readers.

## 5. Data Quality

Check missing values.

Check duplicate records.

Check data types.

Check inconsistent category labels.

Check unusual values.

Check identifier quality.

## 6. Cleaning

Cleaning should be documented.

Do not remove records without understanding why they appear.

Business context should guide cleaning decisions.

## 7. Business Questions

Turn the problem statement into specific questions.

Questions should be measurable when possible.

Each question should connect to available data.

## 8. KPI Selection

KPIs should measure important aspects of the business problem.

Examples include revenue, units, orders and average order value.

## 9. Descriptive Analysis

Summarize the dataset before deeper analysis.

Look at counts, totals, averages, minimums and maximums where relevant.

## 10. Group Analysis

Group data by useful business dimensions.

Product, region and channel are common examples.

## 11. Trend Analysis

Time-based data can reveal changes over periods.

Line charts are commonly useful for trends.

## 12. Visualization

Choose charts according to the question.

Bar charts are useful for category comparison.

Line charts are useful for time trends.

Scatter plots are useful for relationships.

## 13. Insights

An insight should be evidence-based.

Explain what happened.

Then explain why it matters in the business context.

## 14. Presentation

A clear project usually has context, problem, data, method, findings, insights and conclusion.

Keep technical details understandable to the intended audience.

## 15. Validation

Cross-check important calculations.

Compare summary totals with source data.

Review formulas and filters.

## 16. Limitations

Document missing data, assumptions and analytical limitations.

Do not present uncertain conclusions as established facts.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand VBDM project workflow.
- Understand data requirements.
- Plan analysis.
- Plan visualization.
- Prepare business insights.

## 🔎 Project Checklist

Identify the problem.

Understand the data.

Check quality.

Define questions.

Select KPIs.

Analyze.

Visualize.

Interpret.

Present.
