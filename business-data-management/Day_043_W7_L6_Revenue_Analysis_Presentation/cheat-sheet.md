# ⚡ Day 043 Cheat Sheet — Revenue Analysis Presentation

**Author:** Saloni Tiwari  
**Programme:** IIT Madras BS Degree — Diploma Level  
**Day:** 043  
**Topic:** W7_L6 Revenue Analysis Presentation

## 🔑 Core Formulas
Gross Margin = Revenue - Production Cost.
MoM Growth = (Current Revenue - Previous Revenue) / Previous Revenue.
Revenue Share = Segment Revenue / Total Revenue.

## 📊 KPI Set
Total Revenue
Highest Revenue Month
Top Product
Top Region

## 📈 Chart Rules
Time → Line chart.
Categories → Column chart.

## 🧭 Presentation Flow
Question → KPI → Trend → Product → Region → Margin → Insight.

## 🗣️ Storytelling Questions
What happened?
Where did it happen?
How large was the change?
What evidence is needed?

## 🧠 Evidence Levels
Observation → directly supported.
Interpretation → reading of the data.
Hypothesis → possible explanation.
Recommendation → action after evidence.

## 📅 Monthly Analysis
Group by month.
Sort chronologically.
Sum revenue.
Calculate MoM growth.

## 🏷️ Product Analysis
Group by product.
Sum revenue.
Calculate revenue share.
Rank by revenue.

## 🌍 Region Analysis
Group by region.
Sum revenue.
Calculate revenue share.
Rank by revenue.

## 💰 Margin
Revenue alone does not describe profitability.
Compare revenue with production cost.

## 🎤 Slide Design
One main message per slide.
Use clear titles.
Use readable labels.
Avoid unnecessary decoration.

## 📋 Presentation Checklist
Business question defined.
KPI calculated.
Trend chart prepared.
Product breakdown prepared.
Regional breakdown prepared.
Margin context prepared.
Insights written.

## ⚠️ Avoid
Unsupported causal claims.
Unlabelled charts.
Wrong month ordering.
Mixed units.
Overloaded slides.

## 🐍 Python
`groupby()` → aggregation.
`pct_change()` → MoM growth.
`sort_values()` → ranking.
`to_csv()` → export.
