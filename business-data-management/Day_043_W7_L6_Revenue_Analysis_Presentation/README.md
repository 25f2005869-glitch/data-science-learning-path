## 📈 Main Visuals
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue by Product
- Revenue by Region

## 💰 Financial Context
Gross Margin = Revenue - Production Cost.
Revenue should be considered together with cost when discussing financial performance.

## 🧠 Evidence-Based Story
First describe what the data shows.
Then identify possible explanations as hypotheses.
Do not present unsupported causes as established facts.

## 🎤 Core Questions
- What happened to revenue?
- Which month has the highest revenue?
- Which product contributes most?
- Which region contributes most?
- What does gross margin add to the story?
