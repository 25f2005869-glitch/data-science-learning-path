# 📊 Day 068 — Composite Score and Ranking

## 📌 Topic

Day 068 converts multiple candidate assessment measures into a composite score and ranking.

## 🎯 Objectives

1. Understand composite scoring.
2. Apply predefined weights.
3. Calculate a combined score.
4. Rank candidates.
5. Inspect rank bands.
6. Compare departments.
7. Compare experience levels.
8. Compare regions.
9. Separate ranking from selection status.

## 🧩 Scoring Model

Technical Score weight = 30%.

Communication Score weight = 20%.

Interview Score weight = 30%.

Overall Score weight = 20%.

Total weight = 100%.

## 🧮 Formula

Composite Score =
Technical × 0.30 +
Communication × 0.20 +
Interview × 0.30 +
Overall × 0.20.

## 🏆 Ranking

Candidates are ordered by Composite_Score.

The highest composite score receives rank 1.

The model uses descending ranking.

Tied scores receive the same minimum rank in the Python implementation.

## 📁 Dataset

The workbook contains 30 illustrative candidate records.

Departments include Technology, Finance, HR, Sales, and Operations.

Regions include North, South, and West.

Experience levels include Graduate and Experienced.

## ⚠️ Important

The scoring weights are illustrative.

The candidate data is synthetic.

Ranking is dependent on selected variables and weights.

Ranking should not be treated as an independent measure of candidate quality.
