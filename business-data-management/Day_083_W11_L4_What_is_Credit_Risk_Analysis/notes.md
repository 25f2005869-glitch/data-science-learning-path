# 📝 Day 083 Notes — What is Credit Risk Analysis

## 1. Credit Risk

- Credit risk is the possibility of financial loss when a borrower does not meet repayment obligations.
- The analysis begins with defining the borrower, loan and outcome.
- A clear outcome makes the analysis measurable.

## 2. Credit Score

- Credit score provides a compact signal of credit behaviour.
- Higher scores in this dataset represent stronger score bands.
- Score should be analysed alongside other variables.

## 3. Debt-to-Income

- DTI measures debt burden relative to income.
- Higher DTI can provide an important descriptive risk signal.
- DTI alone should not determine a credit decision.

## 4. Delinquency

- Past delinquencies describe previous payment problems.
- Repeated delinquencies can be useful for risk segmentation.
- The dataset records delinquency count as an integer field.

## 5. Loan Exposure

- Loan amount represents financial exposure.
- Exposure concentration can be reviewed by product and region.
- Large exposure and high probability of default are different concepts.

## 6. Default

- Default_Flag is the observed binary outcome.
- Default rate is calculated using customers as the denominator.
- Counts should always accompany rates.

## 7. Risk Segmentation

- Credit score bands provide one segmentation.
- Risk bands provide another descriptive view.
- Product and regional segmentation add business context.

## 8. Risk Score

- The workbook contains an educational composite Risk_Score.
- It combines credit score, DTI, delinquencies and default history.
- It is intentionally illustrative.
- It must not be treated as a production underwriting model.

## 9. Business Interpretation

- Observed differences should be described carefully.
- Subgroup sizes affect rate stability.
- Real decisions require stronger evidence and governance.

## 🔗 Relationship Between Variables

- Credit score is a behavioural credit indicator.
- DTI describes repayment burden relative to income.
- Past delinquency captures historical payment problems.
- Loan exposure describes the amount at risk.
- Default is the observed outcome.

## 🧠 Analytical Thinking

- A high-risk label is an analytical classification, not a final lending decision.
- Multiple dimensions should be examined together.
- Segment-level rates should be interpreted with their counts.

## 🔎 Review Point

- Check the denominator before interpreting any rate.
- Report counts together with percentages.
- Separate descriptive evidence from model decisions.
