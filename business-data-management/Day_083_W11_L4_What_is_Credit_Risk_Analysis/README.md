# 📊 Day 083 — What is Credit Risk Analysis

- Understand the business meaning of credit risk.
- Identify common credit-risk dimensions.
- Analyse borrower and loan-level data.
- Use credit score, DTI and delinquency information.
- Measure observed default rates.
- Create descriptive risk segments.
- Present credit-risk patterns using Excel and Python.

## 🧠 Core Concept

- Credit risk refers to the possibility that a borrower may not meet repayment obligations.
- The analysis focuses on identifying and describing factors associated with repayment risk.
- Credit score is one useful descriptive variable.
- Debt-to-income ratio provides income-burden context.
- Past delinquencies provide behavioural history.
- Loan amount represents financial exposure.
- Default flag is the observed outcome in this educational dataset.

## 🧮 Key Metrics

- Default Rate = Defaults / Customers × 100.
- Average Credit Score summarises the observed score distribution.
- Average DTI summarises debt burden relative to income.
- Loan Exposure is the total loan amount.
- High Risk Share measures the share marked high risk.
- Loan-to-Annual-Income compares requested exposure with annual income.

## ⚠️ Important Limitation

- The dataset is synthetic and educational.
- The composite Risk_Score is an illustrative analytical score.
- It is not a production credit-scoring model.
- It should not be used to approve or reject real borrowers.
- Real credit-risk systems require validated data, governance and monitoring.
