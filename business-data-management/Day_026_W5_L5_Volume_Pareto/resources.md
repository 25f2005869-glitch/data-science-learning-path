## 💻 Excel Resources

Pivot Tables are useful for product aggregation.

Sorting is essential before cumulative calculations.

SUM supports total volume.

Percentage calculations support unit share.

Running totals support cumulative units.

Combo charts can communicate Pareto results.

Use explicit colours for Excel charts.

## 🐍 Python Resources

Pandas supports grouping and aggregation.

groupby() creates product summaries.

sort_values() creates the Pareto order.

cumsum() calculates cumulative volume.

Matplotlib supports charts.

to_csv() stores analysis outputs.

## 🧠 Business Interpretation

Volume concentration can help prioritise operations.

High-volume products may require closer stock attention.

High volume does not automatically mean high profit.

Revenue and units should be analysed separately.

Additional margin and cost data can improve decisions.

## ⚠️ Limitations

This is a learning dataset.

The 80/20 rule is not a universal law.

The dataset does not include product margin.

The dataset does not include inventory availability.

The dataset does not include stockout frequency.
