## 🎯 Objective

Day 026 focuses on Volume Pareto analysis in the e-commerce case study.

The purpose is to identify products that contribute the largest share of unit volume.

The analysis ranks products by Units.

Unit share and cumulative unit share are calculated.

The practical 80% volume group is identified.

## 📌 Core Concept

Pareto analysis ranks categories by contribution.

Volume Pareto uses units as the main measure.

The highest-volume product appears first.

Each product receives a share of total units.

Cumulative share is calculated after sorting.

## 💡 Key Measures

Units are the primary volume measure.

Unit Share = Product Units / Total Units × 100.

Cumulative Units is the running total after ranking.

Cumulative Share = Cumulative Units / Total Units × 100.

The 80% threshold is a practical prioritisation point.
