## 🎯 What Is Volume Pareto?

Volume Pareto ranks products by volume contribution.

In this lesson, volume is represented by Units.

Products are arranged from highest volume to lowest volume.

The contribution of each product is calculated.

Cumulative contribution is then calculated.

## 📊 Unit Share

Unit Share measures the percentage of total units contributed by a product.

Formula: Unit Share = Product Units / Total Units × 100.

A higher share means a larger contribution to total volume.

Shares should normally add to approximately 100%.

The share is based on units rather than revenue.

## 📈 Cumulative Share

Cumulative share is the running total of unit shares.

Products must be sorted by units before calculating it.

The cumulative percentage increases as products are added.

The final cumulative share reaches 100%.

## 🎯 80% Threshold

The Pareto approach often uses an 80% reference point.

The threshold helps identify a practical core group.

The first product that reaches or crosses the threshold is included.

The 80/20 idea is a heuristic rather than a universal rule.

## 💼 Business Meaning

A high-volume product may require stronger inventory attention.

High-volume products can influence warehouse workload.

High-volume products can affect fulfilment planning.

Volume concentration can help prioritise operational effort.

## ⚠️ Important Caution

High volume does not automatically mean high profit.

Revenue and volume are different measures.

Pareto analysis should be combined with other business metrics.

The result should be interpreted in business context.
