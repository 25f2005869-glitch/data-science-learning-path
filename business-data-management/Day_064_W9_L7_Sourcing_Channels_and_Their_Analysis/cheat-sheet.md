# ⚡ Day 064 Cheat Sheet — Sourcing Channels

## Channel Types
Internal Transfer → existing employee moves to another role.
Internal Promotion → existing employee moves to a higher or expanded role.
Employee Referral → candidate introduced by an employee.
Job Portal → candidate sourced through an external portal.

## Core Formulas
Selection Rate = Selected / Candidates.
Selection Rate % = Selection Rate × 100.
Average Cost = Total Sourcing Cost / Candidates.
Cost per Selected = Total Sourcing Cost / Selected.
Average Days = SUM(Days) / Candidates.
Average Satisfaction = SUM(Satisfaction) / Candidates.

## Excel Functions
COUNTIF → channel counts.
SUMIF → channel totals.
AVERAGEIF → channel averages.
COUNTIFS → multi-condition counts.
SUMIFS → multi-condition totals.
PivotTable → channel segmentation.

## Python
groupby() → channel summaries.
agg() → multiple metrics.
mean() → averages.
sum() → totals.
to_csv() → exports.
matplotlib → charts.

## KPI Categories
Volume
Selection
Cost
Time
Satisfaction

## Business Questions
How many candidates came from each channel?
What is the selection rate?
What is the average cost?
What is cost per selected candidate?
How many days does the channel take?
What is average satisfaction?

## Interpretation
Volume alone is insufficient.
Selection rate needs a denominator.
Cost should be considered with outcomes.
Time should be considered with business urgency.
Satisfaction adds another perspective.

## Dashboard
Candidates by Channel.
Selection Rate by Channel.
Average Sourcing Cost.
Average Days to Fill.

## Common Pitfalls
Comparing only volume.
Ignoring denominator differences.
Using total cost without selections.
Ignoring time to fill.
Treating descriptive results as causal.
Forgetting KPI definitions.
