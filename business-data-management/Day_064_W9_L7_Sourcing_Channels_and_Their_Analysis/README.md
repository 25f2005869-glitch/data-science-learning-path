# 📊 Day 064 — Sourcing Channels and Their Analysis

## 📌 Day Overview
Day 064 examines sourcing channels and the business metrics used to compare them.
The package treats each sourcing channel as a category that can be analyzed using volume, selection, cost, time and satisfaction.
The dataset is illustrative and is not official IIT Madras or company data.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
1. Identify common sourcing channels.
2. Understand internal and external sourcing categories.
3. Calculate candidate volume by channel.
4. Calculate selection rate by channel.
5. Compare sourcing cost.
6. Compare time to fill.
7. Compare satisfaction.
8. Build a multi-metric sourcing analysis.

## 🧩 Channels Covered
- Internal Transfer
- Internal Promotion
- Employee Referral
- Job Portal

## 📐 Core Metrics
Candidate Count = number of sourcing records in a channel.
Selection Rate = Selected Candidates / Candidates.
Average Sourcing Cost = Total Channel Cost / Candidates.
Cost per Selected = Total Channel Cost / Selected Candidates.
Average Days to Fill = mean of Days_to_Fill.
Average Satisfaction = mean Satisfaction_Score.

## 💼 Business Questions
How much sourcing volume comes from each channel?
How many candidates are selected from each channel?
What is the selection rate?
What is the average sourcing cost?
How long does each channel take?
How satisfied are stakeholders or candidates?

5. Compare costs.
6. Compare time.
7. Compare satisfaction.
8. Document limitations.

## ⚠️ Interpretation
No single metric fully describes a sourcing channel.
Volume should be read with outcomes and denominator definitions.
Illustrative data should not be presented as organizational evidence.

## 🐍 Python
The Python script reproduces channel, department, region, monthly and role summaries.
It also generates four charts.

## 🧠 Key Takeaway
Sourcing-channel analysis becomes useful when volume, outcomes, cost, time and satisfaction are reviewed together.
