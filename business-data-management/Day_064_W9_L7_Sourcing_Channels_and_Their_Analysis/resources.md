## KPI Framework
Volume measures sourcing activity.
Selection measures conversion to selection.
Cost measures resource use.
Time measures process speed.
Satisfaction adds an experience dimension.

## Data Quality Checklist
Check channel labels.
Check candidate IDs.
Check selected flags.
Check cost units.
Check days-to-fill units.
Check satisfaction scale.
Check month format.

## Analysis Checklist
Define the business question.
Validate the data.
Group by channel.
Calculate volume.
Calculate selection.
Calculate cost.
Calculate time.
Calculate satisfaction.
Compare results.
Document limitations.

## Responsible Analytics
Do not infer channel quality from one metric.
Do not treat illustrative costs as actual organizational costs.
Do not claim causation from descriptive comparisons.
Document KPI definitions.
Protect recruitment information appropriately.
