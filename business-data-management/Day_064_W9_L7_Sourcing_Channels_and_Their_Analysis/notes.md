# 📝 Day 064 Notes — Sourcing Channels and Their Analysis

## 1. Sourcing Channel
A sourcing channel is the route through which candidates enter a recruitment process.
Different channels can produce different volumes, costs and outcomes.

## 2. Internal Transfer
An internal transfer moves an existing employee into another role.
The organization already has employment history and performance context for the employee.

## 3. Internal Promotion
An internal promotion moves an employee into a higher or expanded role.
Promotion analysis can include readiness and role requirements.

## 4. Employee Referral
An employee referral occurs when an existing employee introduces a candidate.
Referral records can be evaluated using common sourcing KPIs.

## 5. Job Portal
A job portal is an external channel through which candidates can discover vacancies.
It can broaden access to external candidates.

## 6. Candidate Volume
Candidate count measures sourcing activity within a channel.
Volume is a basic workload measure.

## 7. Selection Rate
Selection rate measures selected candidates relative to candidates in the channel.
Formula: Selected / Candidates.

## 8. Sourcing Cost
Sourcing cost represents the illustrative cost attached to a sourcing record.
Average and total cost answer different questions.

## 9. Cost per Selected
Cost per selected candidate equals total channel cost divided by selected candidates.
The denominator must be stated clearly.

## 10. Days to Fill
Days to fill represents the time associated with the sourcing record.
Average days can be compared by channel.

## 11. Satisfaction
Satisfaction is an illustrative score that adds an experience dimension.
It should be interpreted using its scale definition.

## 12. Multi-Metric Analysis
Volume, selection, cost, time and satisfaction provide complementary views.
A single metric can produce an incomplete picture.

## 13. Department Analysis
Department summaries show where sourcing activity occurs.
They can support staffing-process reviews.

## 14. Regional Analysis
Region summaries show geographic distribution of sourcing records.

## 15. Monthly Analysis
Monthly summaries help identify changes over time.

## 16. Role Analysis
Role summaries connect sourcing activity to target positions.

## 17. Excel Workflow
Start with Sourcing_Data.
Inspect Channel_Summary.
Use filters for detailed records.
Use the dashboard for presentation.

## 18. Python Workflow
Read the workbook with pandas.
Group records by channel.
Calculate channel KPIs.
Export CSV summaries.
Generate matplotlib charts.

## 19. Interpretation
A high selection rate is only one descriptive measure.
Cost, time and satisfaction add context.

## 20. Summary
Sourcing channels can be treated as measurable business processes.
A consistent KPI framework supports structured analysis.

## 🔬 Channel Comparison
- Compare volume with its denominator.
- Compare selection with cost.
- Compare time with business urgency.
- Review satisfaction with its scale.
- Explain context before drawing conclusions.
